# Manual pass, 2026-11-21: every screen as a user, local stack

Asked by Matheus on 2026-11-21: bring the app up, drive every feature through Playwright as a person would, and look for agreement errors, visual and organisation problems, user errors the app lets through, critical problems and interface improvements. The findings below became Epic 27.

## How it was run

- Local compose stack (podman), `LLM_PROVIDER` and `OCR_PROVIDER` on `fake`; web published on host port 5174 because 5173 was held by another project.
- Personas: `dev@local.dev` (office) and `campo@local.dev` (field) in Empresa Local Dev.
- Viewports: 1024x768 for the walk; a sweep of twelve screens at 390x844 and 1280x800; dark theme on three screens.
- One relatório was created from the template "Cabine primária — padrão" (94 fichas) for Cliente Local, Obra E24 pass, taken to revision 1 (DOCX and PDF, 121 pages) and left in Em revisão.
- Evidence: screenshots under the gitignored `.playwright-mcp/pass-1121/`.

## Coverage

Walked: Login (empty, malformed e-mail, wrong password, sign-in), Home (search, status filters, new relatório dialog), relatório creation, Dados do relatório (six steps), Sumário, Verificações do local (NC with quick phrases, photo, caption, ponto de atenção, bulk Conforme, conclude), one equipment ficha in depth (Disjuntor MT: plate photo and suggestions, readings, conclusion), Fotos, Pontos de atenção, the section text editor, the Gerar dialog (pre-issue list, Conferência por IA, generate), the generated PDF, Cadastros (seven tabs; a client created and removed), Templates (list, one template, one equipment type), Conta, Sincronização, offline edit and reconnect, remove and restore a ficha, remove-local confirmation, sign-out, the second persona.

Not covered: the real camera, real plate reading and the real Conferência por IA (fake providers), dictation (off), the DOCX content, a reload while offline (the dev server has no service worker), drag reordering, the fichas of the other equipment types.

## Findings

Severity: S1 wrong document or wrong data reaches the client; S2 a user error the app lets through or a broken layout; S3 wording; S4 polish.

## What held

Sign-in and its refusals; relatório creation; the NC flow with photo and ponto de atenção; plate suggestions confirmed one by one or all; the outlier remark between phases; "Aprovado" disabled when a reading fails; remove with undo and restore; the revision 2 banner after an edit; offline edit with "1 alteração aguardando" and the send on reconnect; the dark theme; the second persona. No screen overflowed its width at 390, 1024 or 1280 px, and the console carried no application error.

## Decisions of record (Matheus, 2026-11-21)

- All findings are fixed, in three waves: grave, then wording and user errors, then layout.
- The form words of F35 are corrected on screen and in the document (a new seed version; earlier seeds stay frozen).
- Validation: the impossible is refused, the improbable gets an amber remark and a pre-issue row and never blocks.

Where the findings went: Epic 27 in `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md` (Stories 27.1 to 27.14) fixes every finding but three; F27 is Story 26.5, and F22 and F41 are its questions for Bruno.
