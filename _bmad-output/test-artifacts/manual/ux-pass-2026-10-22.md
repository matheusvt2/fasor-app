# Manual UX and coherence pass — 2026-10-22

Hands-on run of the whole product on the local stack (podman compose, `OCR_PROVIDER=fake`, `LLM_PROVIDER=fake`), driven through Playwright MCP as two personas. Focus asked by Matheus: usability and coherence, full flow, status transitions, navigability.

Evidence (gitignored): `.playwright-mcp/ux-pass/*.png` (60 screenshots, numbered in flow order), `.playwright-mcp/ux-pass/preview-rascunho.pdf` (the preview, 17 pages) and `.playwright-mcp/ux-pass/rev1.pdf` (Rev. 1).

## Verdict

The core loop is solid and pleasant: cadastros autosave, template composition, relatório wizard, fichas with batch actions, learned NC phrases, suggestions with confirm, criteria shown inline, out-of-limit flags, não-ensaiado state, pontos de atenção with suggested deadlines, preview and issue through LibreOffice, status transitions in both directions, per-user attribution. The problems below are mostly coherence between surfaces and a handful of real bugs, two of which reach the client document (F41, F42) and one that makes a form field unusable (F6).

## What works well (keep)

Autosave with "N pendentes" → "Sincronizado" everywhere; honest Sync screen ("Último envio de… Este aparelho", "Sincronizar agora"); template editor with variables and "Restaurar texto padrão"; wizard "Concluir" button that names what is missing; ficha header "Faltam N campos…", section strip with counts, "Concluir e avançar" only when complete and returning to the Sumário after the last ficha; batch "Marcar os restantes como Conforme"; learned NC phrases per item; mandatory NC observation; instrument carried over as "Sugerido · último usado"; criterion and source shown next to each test; out-of-limit flags with shortcut; plate reading loop with per-field crops, Confirmar todos, "Criar <fabricante>?"; display reading conflict "Visor: 1,45 GΩ · digitado 12 GΩ — Conferir"; Não ensaiado with reason, read-only fields, automatic section-8 entry; Verificações do local as one screen per local; ponto de atenção with P0–P4 and suggested prazo; preview in a new tab with RASCUNHO watermark; Rev. 1 row with DOCX/PDF; Emitido banner on fichas explaining the reopen, and the reopen itself; "Texto de conclusão desatualizado: TR-1" after a value changed; per-user attribution ("Preenchido por Carla Campos").
