### Process lessons

**P-1. The hands-on behaviour pass ran after two deploys, and found what the tests could not.**
- The playbook asks for it. The integrated review is "two passes. The first is the behavior pass" (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md:359`), a browser pass "over every AC at 390, 768 and 1280 px" (`:280`).
- Wave 1 had one, with no report kept. Waves 2 and 3 record only "the second pass, a code read" (`epic-23-context.md:403`, `:421`). Wave 4 (e23x) merged before wave 3's gate and was read with it.
- Two rows asked for exactly the look that was skipped:
  - the e23w row "look at a v13 transformer's section 9 in the PDF, every word whole", owned by "the wave 2 re-check or wave 3 integrated QA" (`deferred-work.md:3354`);
  - e23z's own "no behavior pass has checked" line (`spec-e23z-final-fixes.md:249`).

  Neither says "before the deploy", so the deploy checklist's `grep` (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md:368`) did not list them.
- The pass ran on 2026-11-10 on the code of `47be2638`, after that code was in production. It failed items 1 and 2 and found two high defects that reach a printed relatório (`behaviour-pass-e23-2026-11-10.md`):
  - F3: the v13 transformer insulation table broke words and a number in print;
  - F2: "Copiar da última visita" copied the oil and winding temperatures, so the second visit was corrected and judged with the first visit's temperature, with no notice.

  It also found F4, F5 and F7. Fix batch e23p and a third deploy followed.
- Every automated gate was green on that code: the final gate on `47be2638` passed 652 of 656 with no red (`epic-23-context.md:431-436`).
- Why the tests missed them:
  - **F3 was judged by an estimate.** e23v reported that the table did not fit by the kernel's own measure (`deferred-work.md:3295`). e23w built Matheus's option (b), the column outside the 60 em budget at 8 pt. The PDF look that would have checked that choice was left as a ledger row. No test read the PDF until e23p added one.
  - **F2 was a seam no story owned.** Story 23.7 added the two temperatures as nameplate fields. The copy chips, built in Epic 5, copy nameplate fields. Story 23.5 corrects by those fields. Each story's tests passed. The epic context has a list of shared files and no cross-story table of assertions, which the playbook asks for (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md:273`, E10-A3).
- Lesson: no deploy of an integrated main before the behaviour pass of its waves has run and its report is tracked. A print change is judged on the PDF. A story that adds a field to an existing entity lists that entity's readers and writers in its DoR (E23-A3).

**P-2. The first deploy went out before the wave's fix batch, and the plan of one deploy became three.**
- The plan: "Deploy. Once, after wave 3's gate, review and fix batch" (`epic-23-context.md:353`); "seed v13 freezes there".
- What happened: `ef099d41` was deployed on 2026-11-09 at 23:56, after wave 3's gate and before e23z. "So the production bundle carries the W3 findings that e23z fixes" (`epic-23-context.md:447`). Two of the seven were confirmed medium (`:421`).
- The deploy checklist was not met on that deploy:
  - `test:e2e:full` on that main had one red, green alone, and its record was written the next day (PR #299). The checklist asks for the record "before the deploy starts" (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md:340`).
  - Two "before the deploy" rows, the paid eval, were still open (P-3).
  - The record does not say on whose word this deploy went. The third deploy's record does.
- What limited the harm:
  - The base import in production was held back until e23z was deployed (`epic-23-context.md:453`).
  - The read-only count found zero company type versions and no stale transformer block (`:454-457`).
  - Seed v13 did freeze. After the first deploy, changes went through code and contract 40, not through the seed.
- What it cost: a second and a third deploy within a day, and R-2, a fix deployed with no contract number.
- Lesson: deploy after the wave's fix batch. If Matheus asks for an earlier one, the deploy record names the known findings and his word before it starts. A fix that changes what a device derives or writes after a deploy states its contract choice in its spec (E23-A3; the e23p case is E23-A2).

**P-3. The paid eval ran after the code it measures was merged and deployed.**
- The rule: a story whose AC depends on what a model perceives "runs its paid eval before its batch PR merges" (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md:381`, E21-A4 P-1).
- Epic 23's own context weakened it to "before the deploy" (`epic-23-context.md:340`). Even that was missed for the first deploy.
- Three stories changed the plate prompt: 23.7 (prompt 6), 23.17 (prompt 7) and 23.18 (prompt 8). Prompt 6 was never measured alone, and no eval of prompt 7 is on record. Prompt 8 was measured on 2026-11-10 at 05:50 UTC, after `ef099d41` was already serving it (`deferred-work.md:3247`; `epic-23-context.md:469`).
- The result was clean: 97,6 % and 98,8 % on the synthetic set with no failed call, 5 of 6 and 6 of 6 on the real set, and every citation valid. So nothing broke. The order was luck, not control.
- Lesson: the rule stands as written. A PR that bumps a reading prompt carries the eval table, or it does not merge (E23-A3).
