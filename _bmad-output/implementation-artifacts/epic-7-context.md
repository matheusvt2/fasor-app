# Epic 7 Context: Generate the FO.SERV-03 relatório

## Goal

Close the loop of the 2026-10-03 slice: an office user sets the parecer, sees every outstanding condition on the Sumário rows and in the Export dialog (exactly one blocking row), previews the document with a RASCUNHO watermark, and issues a numbered revision as DOCX and PDF built from application data. The epic extends the renderer born in Story 4.8 (cover, document control, two-pass TOC, section text 1 to 6 and 10's boilerplate) with sections 7 to 11: the photo record, the points of attention, the equipment sheets as native tables grouped as FO.SERV-03, the parecer and signature block, and the certificates. It also emits the `last_nameplate` projection that feeds "Copiar da última visita" on the next visit.

## Stories

- Story 7.1: Print the equipment sheets as native tables grouped as FO.SERV-03
- Story 7.2: Print the photo record and the photos beside their equipment
- Story 7.3: Print the points of attention and the certificates
- Story 7.4: Set the parecer and print section 10 with the signature block
- Story 7.5: See what is outstanding, preview as RASCUNHO, and issue the revision

## Requirements & Constraints

- Both files render from one frozen snapshot in one server job; the PDF is LibreOffice's conversion of the job's own DOCX, never of an edited one. No upload-a-revised-DOCX path. A failed job changes nothing and consumes no revision number. Any edit after a generation makes the next one N+1; every revision stays listed with number, date, who and both files, frozen including photo numbering. The PDF is stored from day one; its download row ("PDF — enviar ao cliente") is Epic 11's and is not shown.
- Section 7: photos in capture-chronological order, two per row, "Imagem NN: ⟨caption⟩." beneath, stamp line "dd/mm/aaaa hh:mm · lat, lng" (4 decimals, omitted when absent), a checklist-linked photo adds "Item 8 · Contatos · NC"; always the `print` variant. Numbers are provisional before issue and again after any later change.
- Section 8: manual points as bullets in the source's prose form, each `[[foto:id]]` token replaced by "Imagem NN" (numbered in order of first appearance), then derived Não ensaiado entries in tree order with reason and justification. No table, no priority, deadline or owner in the MVP.
- Section 9: native tables (never images), editable in Word. Cabines in tree order; `agrupar_por_tipo` off gives one subsection per cabine in tree order; on gives fixed groups Seccionadoras › Disjuntores › TP's e TC's (per-column pairs) › Transformadores e Cabos de Alimentação (paired by `feeds_block_id`, unpaired cable last with an `integrity` warning); empty groups skipped; para-raios and cabos go inside Seccionadoras (provisional; the seed turns grouping off for every cabine holding them). Titles "⟨Tipo plural⟩ dos Cubículos de MT d⟨o/os⟩ ⟨cabine⟩". Photos linked to a block print inside its sheet. Sub-blocks disabled are omitted; a Não ensaiada sheet prints nameplate and reason band only; unconfirmed Suggestions never print. Conclusion text prints only when `text_status = edited` or `text_basis` equals the current hash. Each value carries its own unit; a mixed column prints "VALORES" with no unit. Labels are sentence case on screen and caps in the document (same seed string, transformed in the renderer).
- Section 10: Parecer box (verdict word as title, confirmed summary), then the three fixed bullets, the validity line "Este relatório tem validade apenas acompanhada da ART ⟨n⟩" or "… da TRT ⟨n⟩" by council, then the signature block (name, "Eng. Eletricista" / "Técnico(a) em Eletrotécnica", registration). No signature image.
- Section 11: the union of instruments referenced by any sheet's copied header and those checked at setup; each certificate prints as full-page images (PDF pages rasterized at 150 dpi with LibreOffice, images through sharp); a `cert_number` mismatch with the registry is an `integrity` flag; a missing file prints a placeholder line and warns at pre-issue.
- Parecer: the app suggests ("Apto?" when every sheet is Aprovado sem restrições; "Apto com restrições?" on any restriction, NC or Não ensaiado), never suggests Não apto, never sets it. The composed summary counts fichas concluídas, com restrições, NC by item, não ensaiadas with reasons and pontos de atenção, with its Criteria line; the user confirms, edits or replaces.
- Pre-issue warns and never blocks, except one row: "Parecer não preenchido". Everything else generates and prints its consequence (untested equipment, expired calibration, uncaptioned photo). Razão social, logo and ART are warnings only.
- No watermark prints on issued documents (FO.SERV-03 has none) and the client logo does not print on the cover; only the preview carries RASCUNHO.
- Traceability: every generated file resolves to its revision and `snapshot_seq`; generation is asynchronous with progress; jobs record duration.
- Testing floor: renderer snapshot test on the Porto Seguro fixture, the draft-equals-issued test (preview and issue differ only by watermark and revision line), and the Sumário/pre-issue identity test (Dexie vs Postgres) on the same fixture.

## Technical Decisions

- One renderer, server-side, over a kernel-typed `RelatorioSnapshot`. `apps/api` renders; every order, number, text, grouping and verdict is a pure kernel function computed once and shared with the device: `groupForPrint(snapshot, scheme)`, `numberPhotos`, `resolveSection8(snapshot, numbering)`, `derivedPoints`, `section11Instruments`, `suggestParecer`, `composeParecer`, `calibrationCheck`, `preIssue`, `editedSince`, `statusTable`.
- `scheme ∈ {por_local_e_tipo, ordem_de_campo}` is stored per relatório (`relatorio/export/scheme`, default `por_local_e_tipo`) and captured in the snapshot; `ordem_de_campo` is the base case the other is built on; `agrupar_por_tipo` applies only inside `por_local_e_tipo`. The Export dialog does not expose it. The renderer never reorders the tree.
- Photo numbers are assigned by `numberPhotos` at generation from the snapshot (sort key `(captured_at, device_id, local_seq)` in the plan; see conflicts) and frozen with the revision.
- Preview is the identical job with `kind: preview`: a RASCUNHO behind-text anchored image on every page, no revision number, a new `file` row of kind `preview` referenced by `relatorio/preview_file_id` (system op), served at `GET /api/relatorios/{id}/preview.pdf`; changes no status; old previews are orphans until the deferred purge.
- Issue: the revision op `revision/{id}` `{number, snapshot_seq, created_by, docx_file_id, pdf_file_id}` is emitted in the transaction that stores both files; in the same commit the job emits `equipment/{id}/last_nameplate = {relatorio_id, revision_number, issued_at, seed_version, block_type, fields}` as `system:generate` for every block with a nameplate. The Export dialog emits `relatorio/status = Emitido`. Status transitions (provisional table): Em campo —generate→ Em revisão (emitted by the dialog, generating while Em campo is warned), Em revisão —issue→ Emitido, Emitido —edit→ Em revisão.
- SM-C1 (confirmed cells later edited) is server-side instrumentation over the ops table.
