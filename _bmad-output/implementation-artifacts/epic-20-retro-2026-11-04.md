### Diff-scope review (three lenses over the unreviewed code)

**R-1. The relatório budget can be bypassed, and it can lock out edits.**
- Confirmed; ADV 1, 2 and 3, ECH 5 and 6, VG 1.
- Where: `value-limits.ts:330-332` `relatorioBudgetVerdict`, and `rules.ts:121-136` `budgetIssue`.
- Problems:
  - (a) "A put of null always applies". But a restore is a put of `removed_at` = null (`ops/apply.test.ts:289`), so removing rows, adding new content and restoring the rows grows a relatório past `RELATORIO_MAX_BYTES` without limit. That recreates the `libreoffice_timeout` the budget exists to prevent.
  - (b) The verdict adds the full new value and never subtracts the value it replaces. The per-push cache also adds every passing put (`rules.ts:134`). So a put that shortens a text in a full relatório is refused, and a long offline session of puts on one field can cross the budget within one push.
  - (c) The stored size is `octet_length(row::text)` of jsonb, which writes `", "` and `": "`. The op size is compact `JSON.stringify`. The two are measured differently, so the real headroom is below the stated 6.5x of Porto Seguro.
  - (d) Plausible: the sum covers every live row with the relatório id, including server rows such as `audit_run` (`http/audit.ts:116`) and generation jobs. Repeated previews and audits therefore spend the user's budget.
  - (e) The in-push growth at `rules.ts:134` has no test (VG 1).
- Disposition: **fix now** (E20-A1). Prevention: a budget rule ships with a test for its restore path and its shrink path (E20-A4).

**R-2. "Reenviar" re-stamps too much, in the wrong order, and can rewrite recorded times.**
- Confirmed; ADV 5 and 6, ECH 1, 2 and 3, plus two claims.
- Where: `sync-store.ts:162-182` and `attribution.ts:270-286`.
- Problems:
  - `RESTAMPED_CODES` holds `op_target_missing`, so every dead target-missing row is stamped to now. That includes rows refused for a target that really is missing and that have no refused create to follow. Under last-writer-wins, an old edit then overrides newer edits by the same user or a colleague.
  - The rows are sorted by their old `client_ts` (`byClientTsThenOpId`), not by commit order. If the clock moved back between a create and its dependents, a dependent is stamped before its create.
  - Value instants within 60 s of the old stamp move by the whole delta to the resend time, not by the device's clock error. So `file.captured_at` and `concluded_by.at` can jump hours or days.
  - The clamp at `attribution.ts:279` also rewrites a far instant later than `at`, which contradicts the function's own doc.
- Disposition: **fix now** (E20-A1).

**R-3. The learned clock offset can go stale, and durations read the corrected clock.**
- Plausible; ADV 7 and 8, ECH 10 and 11.
- Where: `clock.ts:34`, `:49-53`.
- Problems:
  - The offset kept in `localStorage` is applied at boot with no check that the device clock changed since. If a tablet's clock is corrected while it is offline, every op gets the stale offset, and past 10 minutes every op is refused `op_attribution`.
  - Elapsed-time consumers read the corrected `now`: the wake-lock idle (`use-screen-wake-lock.ts:64-66`), the preview and audit "never arrived" expiry, and the reading poll window. A learned jump fires them early or freezes them.
- Disposition: **fix now** (E20-A1). Durations move to a monotonic source. The stale offset is dropped when the raw clock jumps against it.

**R-4. Sign-out can delete unsent work behind the plain dialog, and it leaves keys on paths other than the happy one.**
- (a) Confirmed; ADV 9. `account-surface.tsx:263` sets the unsent count to `db === null ? 0`. A handle that is not open reads as "nada pendente", so the plain "Sair" dialog shows and the wipe deletes any unsent rows on disk. AC 20.11 forbids exactly this. The fix is one line, `null`, which opens the pending dialog.
- (b) Plausible; ADV 10 and ECH 13. `clearUserStorage` runs after three bounded waits and is not part of `runPendingWipes` (`session.tsx:171`, `:390-406`), so a tab closed mid-sign-out leaves the `relatorio-*` keys.
- (c) Plausible; ADV 10 and ECH 13. A geolocation answer that settles after sign-out writes `relatorio-altitude-asked:{id}` again (`altitude-reading.ts:60`).
- Disposition: **fix now** (E20-A1). Item (a) first.

**R-5. N2 is closed only for files filed under the relatório.**
- Confirmed; VG 7, ECH 12, ADV 11.
- Where: `files.ts:343`.
- Problems:
  - A cover photo is filed company-wide (`relatorio_id` null; e20f Design Notes), so it is still served after its relatório is removed.
  - Plausible: `PUT /api/files/:id` (`files.ts:191-240`) still takes bytes for a removed file, or for a file of a removed relatório, and queues a paid reading.
- The issued revision DOCX/PDF stay served by design, an exception the e20f item keeps, so that part is **accepted**.
- The extra query per file GET (ADV 18) is **deferred**.
- Disposition: **fix now** for the cover photo and the PUT (E20-A1).

**R-6. An offline undo across devices is now refused for good.**

It also turns a legitimate offline "Desfazer" into a forward move when another device moved the relatório back in the meantime. That op stays dead, "Reenviar" never clears it, and the sign-out count keeps it as unsent.
- No test pins either ordering.

**R-7. The point editor's close does not wait for every write, and the pick path is unpinned.**

- Disposition: **fix now**, red-first (E20-A1).
