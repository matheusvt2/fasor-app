Checked and found correct (no finding):
- **Variants.** Served sizes are thumb 512 × 384 and print 2000 × 1334 (1500 × 2000 for a portrait). Originals are at most 2560, orientation 6 is applied, and quality is 85.
- **Object key.** `company/{cid}/relatorio/{rid}/photo/{id}`.
- **Photos never block Gerar.** `expectedFileIds` skips `kind: 'photo'`. "Nada impede gerar." shows with 2 uncaptioned photos and a point citing a removed photo.
- **Eviction.** Under a stubbed 180 MB estimate the banner reads "Pouco espaço neste aparelho (180 MB). Sincronize para liberar.". Every acked original was evicted, the viewer then loaded the 2000-px print, and no unacked blob is ever a candidate: `runEviction` needs both `acked` and `uploaded_at`, and re-checks `acked` inside the write.
- **Persistent upload errors.** A `dead` error persists and a `failed` one retries.
- **Server thumbs.** The server thumb replaced the device thumb on pull for all photos.
- **Numbering.** `(captured_at, local_seq, id)` is deterministic across devices; the missing `device_id` is a recorded narrowing.
- **EXIF.** A parse of a GPS JPEG gives −23,5505 / −46,6333 with the pin and "GPS" text.
- **Non-image files.** A non-image file is skipped with "1 arquivo não pôde ser lido como foto e ficou de fora".
- **Derived untested entry.** "Solicitação do cliente — Os ensaios não foram realizados conforme solicitação do cliente.", after the manual points.
- **Reorder.** "Subir" announces "Ponto de atenção movido: 1 de 2 na seção 8" and keeps the focus.
- **Filter.** The filter announces "Mostrando 5 fotos do Cubículo Enel".
- **Viewer.** Esc returns the focus to the tile.
- **No page overflow.** No horizontal scroll at 390 or 768.
- **Carry-over refactors.** The setup split, the Sumário actions, the focus-restore unification and the path builders read as behavior-preserving, and their e2e are green in `full` and `matrix`.
