# Full review 2026-09-30 — front 2: user experience, hands-on

Reviewer: UX agent, driving the running stack (`http://localhost:5173`, Vite dev server, `OCR_PROVIDER=fake`, `LLM_PROVIDER=fake`, `AI_FEATURES` on) as a field engineer would, in a real Chromium (Python Playwright 1.63, headless; the Playwright MCP browser was held by another agent). User `ux@review.test`, company "Revisão UX", seeded with `--standard-template --sample-relatorio`. Every feature was exercised on the sample relatório ("Cliente de Testes Ltda · Local de Testes", 3 blocks) and on a new relatório created from Home ("Cliente UX · Obra UX", 94 blocks from the standard template). Screenshots: `ux/` beside this file (125 files, named in the findings). The machine was shared with three other agents during the whole session, so every timing below is an upper bound; each measurement was repeated three times and the median is reported.

Environment limits, stated so nobody reads them as defects: the dev stack registers no service worker (`navigator.serviceWorker.getRegistrations()` is empty), so an offline *reload* fails at the network level (`ERR_INTERNET_DISCONNECTED`); offline work was tested without reloading, inside the SPA. Headless Chromium has no camera, so the camera was made to fail with `NotFoundError` (the app's own "no camera on this device" branch, `apps/web/src/surfaces/ficha/camera-view.tsx:170`) and every shot went through the system picker with the fixtures in `services/ocr/tests/fixtures/`. The fake plate reading only has a fixture for `transformador_forca`; a seccionadora plate fails by design ("Não foi possível ler").

## 3. Findings, ordered by severity

Each finding: what happens, why it matters, steps, screenshot, the rule it violates, a proposed fix.

### High

**F-01 · A value typed over a plate suggestion is lost when the field is left with Tab or a tap elsewhere.** On TR-7 (a transformer with the fake plate read), TAP atual arrived as "5 · Verificar". Typing `3` and leaving with Tab shows 3 in the field, but nothing is written: the outbox holds no `nameplate/tap_atual` op, and a reload brings "5 · Verificar" back. Only Enter (or the field's own "Confirmar") commits. On the first attempt (TR-6) the typed 3 was silently replaced by 5 before I touched the field again. On a tablet the engineer taps the next field, never Enter, so the correction of exactly the field the app flagged as doubtful disappears. Steps: open a transformer ficha, "Fotografar placa" with `plate-transformador.jpg`, wait for the suggestions, type into "TAP atual", tap another field, reload. Screenshots `22g-placa-digitado-verificar-1280.png` (typed 3 with the "Verificar" pill still attached), `22f-placa-tap-confirmado-1280.png`. Violates EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives ("leaving a field never loses it") and § Smart Input ("typing into a field discards that field's suggestion"). Fix: in `apps/web/src/surfaces/ficha/nameplate-suggestions.tsx` route the suggestion field's text through the same `useFieldCommit` path as the plain fields (`ficha-fields.tsx:30`, blur, Enter or 500 ms idle), so the typed put and the discard land on blur as well.

**F-02 · Undo toasts are anchored to the top of the page, not to the viewport, so on a long sheet the "Desfazer" of a bulk action is never seen.** After "Marcar os restantes como Conforme" on SEC-C05 the toast "12 itens marcados Conforme — Desfazer" rendered at viewport y = −1304 px (page scrolled 1960 px); the same happened on the points page ("Prioridade gravada" drawn over the form, `08c-pontos-prioridade-prazo-1280.png`), on the Sumário ("Dados salvos" over row 6, `18-sumario-novo-1280.png`) and at 390 px on the points page (`08-pontos-390.png`, the toast over the card's Prazo). `.toast` is `position: absolute; bottom: calc(...)` (`apps/web/src/styles/components.css:129`) inside a page that scrolls the document, so it sits near the bottom of the first screenful. Steps: open a seccionadora ficha, scroll to the checklist, tap the bulk action, look for the toast. Screenshot `23b-bulk-conforme-toast-1280.png` (no toast in the viewport). Violates Story 12.1 ("the tap is applied exactly once and its feedback appears (toast…)"), EXPERIENCE.md § Interaction Primitives (undo via a persistent toast), and the mock rule that the toast sits above the sticky bar. Fix: give the app's toast host `position: fixed` (mirroring the mock's absolute-in-frame rule, which assumed the `.screen` was the scroller) and keep `bottom` above the sticky action bar; one rule in `app.css`, no per-surface change.

### Medium

**F-03 · "Restaurar texto do template" restores the seed text, not the template's customised text (audit item 1).** With "2 Definições" customised in the template (bold "[UX-CUSTOM]" prefix, a `{cliente}` variable, an italic " fim"), a relatório created from it shows the customised text; after an edit in the relatório, "Restaurar texto do template" brings back the plain seed paragraph without "[UX-CUSTOM]" or " fim". "Desfazer" on the toast returns the customised text, which shows the restore wrote `null` and the surface fell back to `defaultSectionText`. Steps in section 4. Screenshots `19b-secao-editada-1280.png`, `19c-secao-restaurada-1280.png`. Violates Story 4.7 ("Restaurar texto do template") and § IA › Section text. Fix as the audit states: restore writes the template's `section_text` for that block (`packages/domain/src/relatorio/section-variables.ts:95-96`, `apps/web/src/surfaces/relatorio/section-text-surface.tsx:97-112`), and the button label stays truthful.

**F-04 · Two kernel sentences disagree on the same relatório: the Sumário header says "1 de 3 fichas concluídas", the parecer band says "0 de 3 fichas concluídas".** The sample relatório has one não ensaiada (DJ-TEST): `fichasConcluidasText` (`packages/domain/src/relatorio/progress.ts:141`) counts it as concluded, `parecer.ts:148` does not. The engineer reads both on the same afternoon. Screenshots `04-sumario-1280.png`, `10b-setup-parecer-1280.png`. Violates AGENTS.md ownership rule (one status computed once) and EXPERIENCE.md § IA › Sumário ("one function, two renderings — they can never disagree"). Fix: both texts read `Progress.sheets_concluded` with the not-tested rule decided once (State Patterns says not-tested "counts as complete").

**F-05 · The "Novo relatório" dialog never offers the client's registered sites.** After registering "Cliente UX" with the site "Obra UX" in Cadastros › Clientes, the "Local (obra)" list of Home › "Novo relatório" is empty and only offers "Criar “Obra UX”" after typing the name again (`apps/web/src/surfaces/home/new-project-dialog.tsx:52` builds the list from `projectsOfClient`, never from `client.sites`). Two registries of the same thing, and 7 to 17 extra keystrokes on the office task. Screenshot `17-novo-relatorio-dialog-1280.png`. Violates Story 4.1 (a project "with a client from the registry and a site") and § Registries › Clients ("site(s) with address … Relatório setup (Combobox: select or create)"). Fix: seed the Local list with the client's `sites` and reuse the matching project when one exists.

**F-06 · Bulk actions and copies on a 94-block relatório take 2 to 8 s to show any feedback.** "Marcar os restantes como Conforme" took 7.9 s to flip the rows and show its toast, "Repetir da ficha anterior" 2.6 s, "Igual à SEC-C05?" 2.3 s, ticking an instrument in setup Etapa 4 2.0 s until `aria-checked` changed (the machine was shared, so treat the numbers as upper bounds, but the 3-block sample answered the same taps in under 300 ms). During those seconds the control looks untouched, which is exactly the lost-tap situation Story 12.1 fixed for the race case. Steps: create a relatório from the standard template, open SEC-C05, tap the bulk action. No screenshot can show a delay; the timings are in section 2. Fix: apply the batch optimistically to the sheet's local state (the kernel already computes the result) and let the live query catch up; profile the Dexie live query on `commit` for a 94-block relatório.

**F-07 · "Igual à ⟨TAG⟩?" copies the typed fields but not the two registry-backed ones (Fabricação, Tensão de placa).** SEC-C05 stored Celtta and 15 kV (verified in its DOM after a reload); the copy onto SEC-C06 filled Tipo, Meio de extinção, Corrente, Acionamento and the date, and left Fabricação and Tensão de placa empty, so the engineer retypes the manufacturer on every sheet. `nameplateCopyFields` (`packages/domain/src/relatorio/nameplate-copy.ts:53`) skips only `per_unit` fields, so the loss is upstream: the copied cells for combobox kinds are either not applied by the sheet or not read as filled. Screenshot `24-sec06-instrumento-sugerido-1280.png` (plate after the copy with the two empty comboboxes above). Violates Story 12.3 (D-3: only IDENTIFICAÇÃO, Nº SÉRIE and TAG are per-unit). Fix: a kernel test that copies each of the eight block types with a manufacturer and a voltage class set, then the web side.

**F-08 · The instrument editor of Cadastros is wider than its panel: the certificate row and helper are clipped at 1280 px, and at 390 px the fields run 141 px past the viewport.** At 1280 the "Arquivo do certificado" input, its "Abrir" and "Substituir" buttons and the helper end at x = 1348 in a 480 px panel that ends at 1280 (`15h-cadastros-instrumento-clip-1280.png`, "Ab…" cut); at 390 every field of the panel spans 16 to 531 px (`15-cadastros-390.png`). No horizontal scroll appears because the panel hides its overflow, so the user cannot reach the buttons. Violates DESIGN.md § Layout (gutters, no clipping) and the mock `key-registries.html`. Fix: `min-width: 0` on the grid children of `.registry-panel .field` and `flex-wrap` on the file row (`apps/web/src/surfaces/registries/registries.css`).

**F-09 · A photo reference is inserted glued to the text and without "conforme": "Imagem 1Emoldurar e pendurar …", also in the generated DOCX and PDF.** Choosing a photo in "Escolher fotos" inserts the token at the caret position 0 with no space; the point prints in section 8 and in the action-plan table exactly like that (`relatorio-rev-1.pdf`, line "● Imagem 1Emoldurar e pendurar nas cabines…"). Screenshot `08e-pontos-foto-referenciada-1280.png`. Violates § Capture-to-Document › 8 Pontos de atenção ("conforme Imagem 5"). Fix: insert the token at the end of the text (or the caret) padded with spaces, and let the renderer print "conforme Imagem N" from the token.

**F-10 · The rail's tree rows overlap: "Chave seccionadora" runs under "Em preenchimento".** In the 320 px rail the label column is 87 px wide (76 to 163 px) and the state column starts at 171 px, so "Chave seccionadora" wraps and its last letters sit under the state glyph at 1280 and 768 landscape. Screenshots `05d-ficha-rail-1280.png`, `05-ficha-768.png`, `27b-ficha-banner-conflito-1280.png`. `.tree-body` has `flex: 1; min-width: 0` but the button inside it does not shrink (`apps/web/src/surfaces/relatorio/relatorio-tree.tsx:710-724`, `components.css:374-379`). Violates DESIGN.md § v0.9 direction (row state read before it is read) and the mock `key-relatorio-overview-v09.html`. Fix: `min-width: 0; overflow-wrap: anywhere` on the row button and `flex: none` only on the state, or the state under the TAG on the second line.

**F-11 · Setup Etapa 5 asks the device for its position on every visit while the altitude is empty (audit item 2).** With the permission granted, `getCurrentPosition` ran 4 times per mount (React StrictMode doubles it) and again on every return to the page; on a browser set to "ask each time" this is a prompt per visit. `apps/web/src/surfaces/relatorio/setup/etapa5-local.tsx:40-54`. Violates Story 4.2 AC 2 ("asks once for the site altitude"). Fix: persist a per-relatório "asked" marker (local pref) and stop asking after the first answer, granted or denied.

**F-13 · Photos added from a sheet give no feedback on the sheet, and a plate shot shows nothing for its first 2 to 3 s.** "Adicionar fotos" on SEC-TEST accepted two files, closed its dialog and changed nothing on the ficha (no thumbnail, no toast, no "Fotos" section; the photos were in the gallery with the context caption). **F-14 · The rich text toolbar is clipped at 390 px: "Numeração" and "Variável" wrap to a second row that the 48 px toolbar hides (audit item 5).** The toolbar box measures 288 by 48 px; "Numeração" and "Variável" are laid out at the same x as the first row, i.e. a hidden second row. ## 6. What works well

- The Sumário is the strongest screen: one glance says what is missing, the blocking item is the only red line, and every row is an object with a real menu; "Mover para…" and "Salvar como template" fit it naturally.
- The reading judgement is instant (60 ms) and the helper's wording ("Abaixo do aceitável (>400 MΩ)" with "Marcar Com restrições") tells the engineer what to do; Enter runs through the three tables without a tap.
- The provenance pattern is honest everywhere: "Sugerido", "Verificar", the crop beside the value, nothing written until a tap, "Confirmar todos (9)" saying how many it will take.
- The two-device story holds: the merge is silent and listed, the contradiction is one cell with both values and the display crop, one tap and "Aplicar", undo available.
- Disabled controls carry their reason in text ("Concluir dados do relatório: falta o número da ART", "Sincronizar agora — Sem conexão", "Escolha um motivo"), and every destructive action has a confirm and an undo.
- The generated document is right on the first issue: 13 pages, the parecer box, the action-plan table, the photos with their stamps, and a preview that costs no revision number.
- No console errors, no failed requests, no horizontal overflow on any surface at 1280, 768 or 390, and both themes keep their contrast.
