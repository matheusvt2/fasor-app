### Process lessons

**P-1. The hands-on pass ran before the deploy, twice, and found what the tests could not.**
- Epic 23's main lesson was kept in practice. The early pass ran on `716e26b8` beside fix batch e24x; the final pass ran on `ee0859cc`; both reports were tracked before the deploy (PR #323). The hotfix had its own pass before its deploy.

Fix batch e24y fixed them (`behaviour-pass-e24-2026-11-11.md` Part 1; PR #319).

- **F1 was on record before the pass.** Batch e24p wrote that "GUARD" already breaks in the locked v13 golden and left it as a low row for Matheus (`deferred-work.md:3782`; `spec-e24y-pass-fixes.md:118`). - Lesson: a broken word or number found in a golden or a PDF is a defect row with an owner batch, never a "decision" row. A finding a pass repeats is put to Matheus the first time (E24-A3).

**P-2. The integrated review on fixed commits worked, and each layer of fixes after it had less review.**
- Five read-only passes on `b07d3397`, `e5fdde82` and `52ddc91a` found D1 (high), six medium and fourteen low findings while the last batches were still building, and all went to one fix batch (`epic-24-context.md` § Integrated review).

- Lesson: the last commit before a deploy that changes product code gets a read. A test of an editor types into it and waits past its autosave (E24-A3).
