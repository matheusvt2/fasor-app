### Test time and gate throughput (known finding, recorded on Matheus's request)

**F-1 The merge gate runs 38 to 46 % over its 900 s budget, and the queue in front of it costs more than the gate.** Numbers:

- `pnpm verify`: 1248-1318 s against the 900 s DoD budget (coordinator notes B-1; QA report "Gate timings": 1318 s with lint 31.8 s, static 48.9 s, test:api 213.5 s, test:unit 179.7 s, test:e2e 920.2 s; PR #55: 1258 s).
- e2e `@p0` alone: 868-1072 s on one worker, about 7 s per test (B-1; `spec-test-speed.md:97`: 868 s, parallel 635 s, serial 229 s).
- `test:e2e:full`: about 26 min (1560-1585 s; QA report: 228 tests, parallel 190 in 992 s, serial 38 in 561 s).
- Gate queue: with one gate at a time under `flock /tmp/fasor-verify.lock`, waiting for the lock cost 10-35 min per batch several times (B-1). PR #51 alone needed four gate runs (B-4).
- Batch T (PR #53) cut verify from 1499 s to 1268 s (`spec-test-speed.md:111`); the gate remains over budget.

Disposition: fix now (E7-A1, E7-A2). Upstream lesson: the 900 s budget in DoD clause 3 has not held since Epic 6 (E6-A1 measured 17-21 min); a budget that is breached every run is not a gate. Either the gate meets it or the DoD states the real number with a dated line.

**F-2 A long timeout hid a simple failure.** A click on an `aria-disabled` "Gerar relatório" waited the full 360 s test timeout before failing (7.2-E2E-001), until PR #53 set `actionTimeout` 15 s, `navigationTimeout` 30 s, `expect` 5 s (B-2; `spec-test-speed.md:79`). Disposition: accept (fixed by #53). Lesson: fail-fast defaults stay in `playwright.config.ts`; a spec that needs more states it locally.

**F-3 Web unit files flake under load.** `template-composer-undo`, `setup-surface` and `relatorio-tree-edits` timed out whenever development ran beside a gate; the gates of batches S and P each failed once on them (B-3). `test:unit` beside the other stages ran jsdom files three times slower and timed out 7 tests, so #53 gave it a phase of its own (`spec-test-speed.md:73-76`). Disposition: fix now (E7-A2): the files still time out when a dev batch runs beside the gate.

**F-4 Three workers still fail one timing test per run.** With the push fix of PR #48, `test:e2e:full --workers=3` still fails one test per run; in #53's validation `verify` #1 at 3 workers failed 12.4-E2E-001 (load 12-14) and 12.1-E2E-007 (serial group), so `PARALLEL_WORKERS` stays 1 (`spec-test-speed.md:96-99`, `:118-124`; deferred-work entry of `spec-epic-8-carry-over.md`). Disposition: fix now through E7-A1 (3 workers is the second lever once the device cost drops).

**F-5 The next lever is the device-side snapshot rebuild per commit.** Every commit rebuilds the relatório's whole snapshot through its live query (`toSnapshot` + `buildSnapshot`, about 223 ops of a standard relatório; `apps/web/src/db/snapshot.ts`), so under CPU contention a commit's render lands late (B-6; deferred-work entry of `spec-test-speed.md`, owner Epic 9 carry-over batch). This is the measured cause behind F-4 and the per-step cost behind F-1. Disposition: fix now (E7-A1).

**F-6 The gate depends on the npm registry at start.** The first `test:e2e:matrix` attempt died after 32 s on a network timeout downloading the pnpm 12.5.1 binary inside `tools` (QA report, gate table). Disposition: fix now, low (E7-A7).
