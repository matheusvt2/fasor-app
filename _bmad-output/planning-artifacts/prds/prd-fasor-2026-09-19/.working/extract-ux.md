## 1. User journeys

The UX docs define journeys as **Key Flows**, numbered Flow 1 to Flow 6 with one lettered sub-flow (Flow 2b). There are no UJ-N identifiers anywhere in the sources; `review-rubric.md` confirms "Sources carry no named UJs or FR IDs". **The PRD must mirror these IDs exactly as `Flow 1`, `Flow 2`, `Flow 2b`, `Flow 3`, `Flow 4`, `Flow 5`, `Flow 6`.** Each flow has a named protagonist (Bruno / Eduardo), numbered steps, one explicit **Climax** beat, and a **Failure** paragraph (Flow 6 is itself a failure path with a nested failure).

Modal stacks one level deep. **No hamburger drawer; Home is two taps from anywhere** (App bar wordmark). Office versus field is a matter of surface, not device.

**Block Model (object model behind the IA).** Organizing principle, verbatim from the user: the laudo is *"como se fosse um draw.io com objetos prontos em que o usuário pode adicionar ou remover"*.
- **Template**: reusable ordered composition of blocks with quantities and sub-block defaults, **carrying the location skeleton** (cabines and columns with equipment quantities per column) so every block of a new laudo is born in place with its final suggested TAG. Not tied to a project. Edited in Template composer (office).
- **Laudo**: an instance of a Template inside a Project, for one visit. Owns filled data, photos, points of attention, export history.
- **Block (coarse)**: a whole section or a whole equipment sheet; addable, removable, reorderable. **Section block** (FO.SERV-03 sections 1–6, 8, 10, 11 — mostly fixed text with a few variables; boilerplate edited with the Rich text editor in the Template composer only) and **Equipment block** (one sheet, one equipment, one TAG — mandatory at creation, suggested automatically).
- **Sub-block (fine)**: a test, a checklist item, a nameplate group, the observation box. Template defines defaults per block type/subtype; a Laudo may override per sheet. A sub-block toggled off is omitted from print, never printed empty. Checklist items are never removed: subtype NA defaults premark rows and remain editable.
- **Location**: Cabine › Coluna/Cubículo. **The Cabine owns** its substation characteristics (type, voltages, power), its test environment (temperature, humidity, altitude) and the flag *Agrupar por tipo*. **One editing place**: the block labeled **"Da cabine"** on the cabine's first sheet; every other sheet shows the values read-only under the same label; the cabine tree row shows them read-only. Printed once per cabine on its first sheet.
- **The 8 equipment block types** (pt-BR labels; the brief counts 6 because it groups cables and TP/TC): Cabos de entrada (no nameplate; 5 checklist items; Isolação Fase A/B/C/Reserva × Massa) · Para-raio entrada/saída (6 nameplate fields; 5 items; Isolação A/B/C/Reserva) · Chave seccionadora (9 fields; 14 items; Isolação contato aberto T1/T2, T3/T4, T5/T6 + contato fechado A/B/C · Resistência de contato T1-T2, T3-T4, T5-T6) · Disjuntor MT (12 fields incl. Relação, Exatidão; 15 items; Isolação R/S/T · Relação P1-P2 / S1-S2) · Cabos de saída / alimentação TRn (no nameplate; 5 items; Isolação A/B/C/Reserva) · Transformador de força (12 fields; 15 items; Isolação 3 combinations Primário/Secundário/Massa · Relação por TAP; **conclusion restored** — missing today on all 8 transformer sheets).

## 3. Interaction and behavior rules (from EXPERIENCE.md)

**Foundation.** Responsive web app, **tablet-first**, phone-capable, desktop for office composition and review. No native apps, no app-store publishing. Two contexts, one app: *Office* (desktop/notebook, connected) composes templates, sets up the laudo, reviews, exports; *Field* (tablet/phone, usually no signal) fills sheets, photographs, adds or removes the odd block. Composing is the office rule and the field exception; filling is field work. Sign-in required and simple; named users (Bruno, Eduardo); every sheet records who filled it and when. All users of the company see all projects and laudos; no roles, no per-laudo sharing in the MVP; one user per device session; users provisioned outside the app. Theme follows the device preference with a manual override in Account (Sistema / Claro / Escuro). Must run on Android Chrome, iPadOS Safari and desktop Chrome/Edge. UI system TBD by architecture.

**Interaction primitives.**
- Tap to act; one primary action per surface; no hover-only affordances.
- **Autosave** on every field change (debounce = architecture's call) to local storage, then to the server when online. No Save button on sheets; "Concluir ficha" marks state, it does not save. A visually hidden "Salvo" status is announced at most every few seconds, never per keystroke.
- **Reorder three ways, always:** press and hold 300 ms + drag; Overflow menu "Subir · Descer" (and "Mover para…" where a location can change); keyboard Alt+↑/↓ wherever the element is focusable and a keyboard is attached. The new position is announced ("SEC-C09 movido para a posição 3 de 5"). No swipe gestures (gloves, glare) except system back.
- **Enter moves down a measurement column; Tab moves right.** Numeric fields use `inputmode="decimal"`.
- Next/previous sheet follows tree order; "Próxima coluna" jumps locations.
- Camera or copy before keyboard; the keyboard is a fallback reached by a text link.
- Photos: camera opens directly; burst until "Concluir fotos"; caption defaults silently; numbering is never manual.
- Bulk over per-row, never silent; no row is pre-marked Conforme by the app.
- Suggestions are never writes (see section 4).
- **Undo** for destructive-but-recoverable actions via a persistent Toast action ("Ficha removida — Desfazer") that stays until dismissed or until the next navigation, at least 20 s; afterwards "Restaurar ficha removida" in the Laudo overview Overflow menu until export.
- **Banned:** carousels, infinite scroll (registries paginated), auto-decisions on verdicts, pre-marked Conforme, AI values written without a tap, a modal choice before the camera, modal stacks > 1, color-only state, tooltips as the only explanation, clearing a selection by re-tapping it, explanatory lines repeated on every sheet.

**Feedback surfaces.**
- **Toast**: one at a time, 6 s, persistent when it carries an action; undo toasts stay ≥ 20 s; never for errors that need a decision; `role="status"`.
- **Banner**: non-blocking, **one slot per surface**, fixed priority **conflict › draft found › suggestions ready › laudo exported › offline**; other conditions fold into the Sync badge and a "+2" chip on the banner that opens Sync status. Variants: warning (calibration expired, storage low, laudo already exported), conflict (`role="alert"`), info (install prompt, "Sugestões prontas"). Dismissible per session; returns while the condition holds. Offline alone is a Toast once per session plus the badge, not a banner.
- **Sync badge**: states `ok` / `pending` / `offline` / `error` / `conflict`; always with a word ("Sincronizado", "3 pendentes", "Sem conexão", "Erro", "Conflito"); updates live; never blocks input; announces state *transitions* only, never counts.
- **Progress counter**: counts required fields still empty (checklist rows unset, required measurements, conclusion, required observation); **unconfirmed suggestions count as empty**; "Completa" at zero; never blocks navigation.
- **Section stepper**: four steps Placa · Verificações · Ensaios · Conclusão with missing counts; tapping scrolls and expands; completed sections collapse to their title line; "Concluir ficha" with missing items jumps to the first missing field. Not a wizard: every section editable at any time.

**Confirmation patterns.** Confirm dialog for removing blocks, changing status backwards, discarding conflict versions, sign out with pending sync: one sentence stating the consequence, the action verb on the button ("Remover ficha", not "OK"), initial focus on "Cancelar", Esc/back cancels, destructive action drawn as a red outline never a red fill. Destructive actions always route through a Confirm dialog. Buttons are disabled until valid, with the reason shown as `label` text beside them (`aria-describedby`, still focusable), never a tooltip.

**Validation and non-blocking rules.** Out-of-limit values, magnitude outliers, expired calibration, missing required fields and offline state never block navigation. The one blocking rule in the whole product is **"Parecer não preenchido" blocks "Gerar laudo"**; everything else in the pre-issue list is informative (a missing logo prints the razão social instead). *Com restrições* makes the Observation required; *Sem restrições* with any NC row shows an inline warning ("Há itens não conformes") but is allowed. "Concluir ficha" requires Progress = Completa; Não ensaiada counts as complete and is not listed as "não concluída" at export.

**Offline behavior.**
- **Local first**: every write lands on the device before anything else; the UI never waits on the network. Photos are kept as captured and never removed locally until the server acknowledges them.
- **Automatic availability**: every laudo in *Rascunho* or *Em campo* is downloaded to the device (data, template, registries, photos at thumbnail and full size [ASSUMPTION]) whenever a connection exists, without the user asking. Laudo card says "No aparelho · atualizado 21:40" / "Baixando… 12 de 94 fichas · 8 de 31 fotos" / "Não está neste aparelho — conecte para baixar" (card grayed; tap explains in a Toast and does nothing else; never a blank screen or a spinner). *Em revisão* and *Emitido* laudos download on open.
- Offline write: save locally immediately, Sync badge `pending`, no modal, no banner per save.
- Session persists on the device so the field user is not signed out offline; session expired while offline → keep working, re-authenticate on next successful connection before sync, without discarding the queue. Cold open without session and offline → the Login form explains "Sem conexão — entre quando houver sinal; os laudos já baixados neste aparelho continuam disponíveis após o login" and does not spin.
- Storage low → global Banner "Pouco espaço neste aparelho (⟨n⟩ MB). Sincronize para liberar."; capture still allowed until the browser refuses; a refusal is an error Toast and, when online, the photo is uploaded immediately instead of stored, otherwise kept for one retry.
- PWA install is the mechanism for cold-open offline and durable storage; the UX requires only the one-time install Banner ("Instale PRODUTO na tela inicial para usar sem conexão" with "Instalar / Agora não", repeatable only from Account) and that the app keeps working uninstalled while online.
- Export needs a connection (server-side generation assumed); the dialog says so when offline.

**Sync behavior.**
- Background sync when a connection appears **and while the app is open** (background execution is not assumed); sheet data first, photos second; retry automatic.
- **Visible state always**: Sync badge in the App bar; Sync status lists uploads pending, downloads in progress, last successful sync, errors, conflicts and per-user "Último envio de Eduardo: 06/09 18:10" — the server cannot know what is still on another device, so the last send time is the honest signal and the export pre-issue list repeats it.
- **Conflict model, per entity.** *Sheet body* (nameplate, checklist, tests, observations, conclusion, not-tested state, photo links) is **merged by sub-block with fixed rules**: a filled value beats an empty one; **NC beats C**; a photo link is never dropped; free text (observations, reasons) latest edit wins and the other version stays readable in Sync status for the session. Everything merged is listed as information, never asked. Only a **true contradiction** — two different filled values in the same cell (two readings, two conclusions, C vs NA) — opens the Conflict view, with a **per-cell pick** (source crop beside an OCR'd value). *Laudo structure* (block add/move/reorder, locations and cabine data, captions, points of attention, setup) merges automatically and is listed as information; exactly two cases ask: **deletion vs. edit** of the same block ("SEC-C15: removido por Eduardo, alterado por você — Manter / Remover") and a **duplicate TAG** created on two devices ("SEC-C09 foi criada em dois aparelhos — Renomear uma / Manter as duas"; Manter keeps both with a "-2" suffix on the later one). Two devices editing different sheets of the same laudo never conflict — the intended way to split work.
- **Conflict view** lists only the contradicting cells, one 56px+ row each, two option rows per cell with value, author, time and the 48px source crop where a reading exists; a meta line at the top says "O resto da ficha já foi mesclado"; "Aplicar" writes the picks; Esc/back returns without deciding. Structure conflicts show one block card per side with "Manter" · "Remover".
- **Never lose a photo** is the acceptance bar.

**Failure handling.** Photo upload rejected → red pill "Erro — Tentar novamente" under the tile, the pill is the retry button, the photo stays on the device, other photos continue. Sync error → badge `error` with the failing item listed and "Tentar novamente"; other items keep syncing. Generation failed → inline error with "Tentar novamente"; laudo data untouched; no revision number consumed. Reading failed → "Não foi possível ler — Tentar novamente / Preencher manualmente" under the photo; the photo is kept; nothing was written. Camera/file permission denied → inline reason with the OS path under the Camera capture button and the gallery link as the fallback; never a silent no-op. Dictation unavailable → the button is **hidden** (not disabled); chips and keyboard remain. Location denied → photos keep the time stamp only, no placeholder, and the Account row shows "Permissão negada no aparelho" with the OS path, switch stays on.

**Status lifecycle.** Rascunho → Em campo (first sheet touched) → Em revisão (user sets) → Emitido (on export). Backward moves allowed via Confirm dialog. A full (state, event) transition table is an open question.

**Voice and tone.** Register of the sheet itself: short, technical, sentence case, no exclamation marks, no encouragement, normative vocabulary where it gives credibility (NR-10, PIE, RBC). Placeholders show a real example ("Ex: 13,8 kV", "Ex: 2E"). pt-BR numbers (decimal comma, "17,5 kV", "3.700 MΩ"), dates dd/mm. Vocabulary fixed: *laudo* (document), *ficha* (equipment sheet), *bloco* (composable unit), *cabine / coluna / cubículo* (locations). **No emoji anywhere in the product** — icons come from the SVG sprite. Representative Do/Don't pairs: "Sem conexão. Tudo fica salvo neste aparelho." not "Erro de rede"; "Não ensaiado — motivo" not "Pulado"; "Abaixo do aceitável (>400 MΩ). Marcar Com restrições?" not "Valor inválido"; "Calibração do 2E vencida em 28/08/2027. Pode continuar." not a blocking dialog; "Eduardo marcou o item 10 como Não conforme, com foto — mesclado" not "Conflito de versão (409)"; "Fase A, 1 minuto: 330 MΩ (você) · 3.300 MΩ (Eduardo) — escolher" not "Manter a minha / Manter a dele" for a whole sheet.

**Responsive rules.** Phone (< 768px): single column everywhere; Laudo tree is a surface, not a rail; measurement tables become stacked per-row cards; Block palette is a bottom sheet; Sticky action bar full-width; camera capture is the primary photo path. Tablet (768–1279px, primary): persistent left rail (320px) + sheet column inside a laudo; portrait collapses the rail to a 48px toggle strip and the action-bar buttons fill the width; Block palette is a right drawer; landscape shows measurement tables in full. Desktop (≥ 1280px): as tablet landscape plus keyboard shortcuts; Template composer shows palette and composition side by side; Export and Registries prefer this width.

## 4. AI / assist interaction contract

**Where AI runs.** In the backend, always (memlog decision); nothing runs on the device. All AI is subject to the provenance-and-confirm pattern below. The **generated conclusion text and the parecer summary are not AI**: they are templates filled with values already on the device, so they work in the cubicle without signal and are excluded from the reading queue.

**Offline.** The photo is saved and queued with the line **"Foto guardada — leitura quando houver sinal"** under it; the reading starts at the first signal (gate, van, hotspot) **while the app is open**, and a Toast **"3 leituras prontas para confirmar"** (action "Ver") opens the first sheet with pending suggestions — so the engineer who took the photo is still on site to check it. If the signal never comes before the office, the crop beside each value keeps the check possible on Monday. A sheet opened later with suggestions still pending shows the info Banner "Sugestões prontas". Photos taken for a reading (plate, display, panel) go **first in the upload order**. The Sync status headline counts "leituras na fila" and "sugestões por confirmar".

**Provenance-and-confirm pattern — shared by every assist.** A value the engineer did not enter is rendered by the **Suggestion field**: `{colors.fora-do-limite-fill}` amber background, a **"Sugerido"** pill and, for OCR and vision, the **source crop** as a 48px thumbnail beside the value (tap to zoom the region on the photo; for a nameplate, each field's region is outlined on the inline plate crop while that field is focused). **"Confirmar"** per field (≥ 48px). **"Confirmar todos"** confirms every "Sugerido" field of the group and **skips every field flagged "Verificar"** — an ungrounded or low-confidence guess, shown with its best guess rather than blank (an editable guess costs a glance, a blank costs typing) and confirmable only by a tap on that field itself; the button label counts ("Confirmar 7"). "Verificar" is drawn on a 2px **dashed** amber border to separate it from "Sugerido".
- **Nothing unconfirmed is written, counted by the Progress counter, or printed.**
- Typing into a suggested field **discards that field's suggestion** (that field only; for a queued display shot, typing cancels that shot's suggestion for that cell only).
- A field already filled by the engineer is **never overwritten**: a differing suggestion appears beside the value as a meta line "Sugerido: 15 kV — Substituir".
- On confirm the field returns to the neutral filled style; the crop shrinks to a 24px glyph in the suffix slot and **stays reachable until export**.
- Announced to screen readers as "Sugerido, 147 gigaohms, confirmar"; crop alt text "Recorte do visor" / "Recorte da placa".

**Wrong-digit protection.** A reading is accepted only when its digits appear in the **OCR text layer** of the crop (no LLM-only numbers); a reading whose digits are not in the OCR text layer is flagged "Verificar". **Magnitude outliers** within a table (≥ 100× apart) are flagged with an amber helper line on a neutral field ("Fase C 1000× abaixo de A e B. Conferir?") — a hint, never a block. **Nameplate values are cross-checked against Registries**: the voltage class must exist or the field is flagged "Verificar"; an unknown manufacturer asks "Criar Celtta?" inline.

**Copies and defaults are not suggestions.** Copies and defaults (previous visit, previous cabine, previous row's unit, last reason used, context caption) are written as **plain values with an undo Toast** ("Copiado de SEC-C04 — Desfazer"), because their source is the engineer's own earlier entry.

**Never auto-written / never suggested.**
- **No verdict is ever auto-decided.** The app calculates, compares and highlights; the engineer concludes. **Reprovado is never suggested** at sheet level; **Não apto is never suggested** at laudo level.
- Checklist rows are **never pre-marked Conforme silently**.
- An out-of-limit value is amber (suggestion), never red (red is reserved for what the engineer marked NC / Reprovado); the app suggests "Marcar Com restrições" and only sets the Conclusion control if tapped.
- Vision proposing NC rows from the sheet's photos is explicitly **not in the MVP**.
- Context captions are defaults derived from the engineer's own position on the sheet (not read from the image) and are written as plain editable captions; **vision captions are suggestions**.
- A photo that vision cannot caption stays "Sem legenda" and is listed at export, **never guessed into a caption**.

**AI-related states** (State Patterns):
- *Reading in progress (online)*: inline "Lendo…" line in `meta` under the photo/tile; fields stay editable — a field typed meanwhile is excluded from the incoming suggestions.
- *Photo queued for reading (offline)*: meta line "Foto guardada — leitura quando houver sinal" under the plate tile / measurement cell / photo tile; the photo is queued as a normal photo (readings first in the upload order); no Banner; the cell stays typeable.
- *Reading arrived*: on the first signal the queued readings run; Toast "3 leituras prontas para confirmar" with the action "Ver"; the Sync status headline counts them; a later-opened sheet shows the info Banner "Sugestões prontas".
- *Reading failed*: "Não foi possível ler — Tentar novamente / Preencher manualmente" under the photo; the photo is kept; nothing was written.
- *Suggestions pending*: amber fields with "Sugerido"; source crop beside OCR/vision values; ungrounded or cross-check-failed fields carry "Verificar" with the guess shown; "Confirmar" per field / "Confirmar todos" (skips "Verificar"); unconfirmed suggestions are not counted as filled and never print.

**Dictation contract.** Uses the device's speech engine (pt-BR); while listening the button shows "Ouvindo…" (no pulsing color alone; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected); the result **is a suggestion**, never written until confirmed. On a Measurement table the utterance is parsed to row + value + unit ("Fase A, 147 giga" → Fase A: 147 GΩ); unparsed speech lands in the Observation as text to confirm. **Hidden, not disabled,** when the engine is unavailable (no browser support, no offline pt-BR pack); chips and keyboard remain. Accessible name "Ditar observação"; announces "Ouvindo…" and then the suggestion text.

**Queue order for the assists** (report generation still ships first; this inverts the brief's "AI last in the queue" under the user mandate of 2026-09-18):
1. Camera-first nameplate (LLM vision) with "Copiar da última visita".
2. Display OCR for readings ("Ler visor").
3. Auto-captions (context first, vision on sync).
4. NC observation drafting from the row's photo.
5. Equipment / column detection ("Fotografar equipamento").

**Volume drivers for cost estimation** (stated in the spine and the adversarial review): ≈ 94 plates + ≈ 1.100 display shots + 82 captions per job.

## 5. Capture ergonomics

- **Interaction budget (hard design target).** "Type as little as possible: the app captures by camera and infers; the engineer confirms." A fully conforme seccionadora with a copied nameplate must be completable in **≤ 20 taps and ≤ 15 keystrokes** (the brief's success criterion is ≤ 20 keystrokes per sheet; this spine sets the tighter design budget). **Any field not fillable by tap, photo, copy or default is an exception this spine must justify.** Today's stated exceptions: the free text of an NC observation when no chip fits and dictation is unavailable; a TAG that differs from the suggestion; a new registry entry ("Criar Celtta?" still needs the name typed once). Flow 2 counts a real sheet against the budget: 30 taps / 0 keystrokes for an NC sheet with two photos and a dictated observation, 19 taps / 0 keystrokes for the budget case.
- **Simplicity mandate (user decision, 2026-09-18):** "Simplicity and ease of use above completeness: every field, screen and step must justify its cost to the engineer." Applied: the priority fills the deadline instead of adding a field; the council choice is one tap prefilled from the profile; the document control table is composed from data already entered; the generated conclusion is one confirming tap; the company profile is once per company with two required fields (razão social, logo); the photo stamp costs nothing at capture.
- **Field context [ASSUMPTION — unconfirmed with Bruno]:** touch with leather gloves (luva de vaqueta) possible; frequent one-handed use; variable light (dark indoor cubicles, direct sun outdoors); **zero signal inside the substation**; one device per laudo, with a possible second executor on a different sheet from another device.
- **Glove sizing.** `touch-min` **48px** minimum hit area for anything tappable; `touch-field` **56px** for controls used with gloves on — tri-state segments, measurement fields, conclusion segments, tree rows, palette items. Every element with a tap behavior has ≥ 48×48px even when its glyph is smaller (drag handles, overflow buttons, tree chevrons, Sync badge, App bar back and avatar, the unit control, quick chips, text buttons, toast actions, the "Tap TAG" affordance, the rail toggle); padding or a pseudo-element supplies the difference. Decorative marks (palette "+", photo number badge, state glyphs) are not interactive.
- **One-handed reach.** The primary action sits in the Sticky action bar, which fills the width on phone and tablet portrait (right-aligned only on tablet landscape/desktop, where it docks to the content column bottom on desktop). The bar sits **above** the on-screen keyboard, never under it, and unsticks when the viewport is shorter than 480px. Focus is never hidden under the bar or the keyboard (`scroll-padding` = bar height + keyboard inset).
- **No swipe gestures** (gloves, glare) except system back. No hover-only affordances. Reorder never requires dragging.
- **Sunlight and dim cubicles.** High contrast, no mid-tones carrying meaning; every semantic color paired with a word or letter; shadows only on floating sheets and dialogs (a soft shadow disappears in sun, a 1–2px border does not); both light and dark themes obey the same sunlight rules, because a tablet set to dark is still carried outdoors and a light screen at full brightness is itself glare in a dim cubicle; theme follows the system preference with a one-tap override in Account.
- **Keystroke minimization mechanisms:** camera-first nameplate; "Ler visor" display OCR with burst; dictation; unit typed as a suffix ("147G") and unit tap-cycle toggle defaulted from the previous row; chip rows of the 5 most recent values instead of comboboxes on field surfaces; example placeholders; Enter moves down a measurement column.
- **Bulk actions:** "Marcar os restantes como Conforme" (head of the checklist and mirrored in the Sticky action bar) and "Repetir da ficha anterior do mesmo tipo"; both one tap with a persistent undo Toast; both disabled with a visible reason when not applicable. "Confirmar todos" / "Confirmar 7" per suggestion group. "Confirmar todas" for 12 vision captions in the gallery header and the export pre-issue list.
- **Copy-from-previous:** "Copiar da última visita (SEC-ENT)" (same TAG in a previous laudo of the same site) and "Igual à SEC-C04?" (same type and model already plated in this laudo) above an empty nameplate; "Copiar da cabine anterior" chip for temperature and humidity; the previous row's unit as the default; the last Não ensaiado reason preselected; the last instrument used per test type remembered within the laudo; the last template used in the project as the default in "Novo laudo".
- **Smart defaults:** altitude prefilled from geolocation, confirmed once per site; responsável and council prefilled from Account › Registro profissional; caption generated silently from context; report type preselected; the section 9 choice remembered per laudo and hidden under "Opções" unless it differs from the template default; suggested TAG from type + column; deadline from priority; "Continuar" pointing at the last sheet edited on this device (else the first incomplete sheet in tree order).
- **Hint-text discipline** (a scan-speed decision): no explanatory line repeats from sheet to sheet — the checklist legend shows once per session then behind a "?" button; the criterion source ("aceitável na ficha") and the instrument's serial/RBC sit behind a chevron; "Obrigatório" exists only as the Progress counter and the amber helper.
- **Navigation cost:** Continue card hero on Home; App bar back from a sheet returns to the tree scrolled to that row; "Próxima ficha" / "Próxima coluna"; the Section stepper jumps to the missing field; "Concluir ficha" with missing items jumps to the first one.

## 6. Design decisions with rationale

- **Brand and tone.** The product has no visual identity; wherever the name would appear the UI shows the literal placeholder **PRODUTO**. The look is "a well-printed test sheet, not a consumer app": white paper, black ink, one deep blue for actions, and a small set of semantic colors that mean exactly one thing each. Rationale: the job is to replace a handwritten A4 sheet filled inside a de-energized medium-voltage substation and to produce FO.SERV-03 the same day.
- **Sober colors chosen for sunlight readability** (the one constraint the user set): high contrast, no reliance on mid-tones; **every semantic color paired with a text label or icon** so meaning survives a washed-out screen. GroundPRO's dark-green theme is admired but explicitly **not** the direction.
- **Both themes required**, following `prefers-color-scheme` with a manual override (Sistema / Claro / Escuro) in Account; dark is not exempt from the outdoor contrast bar.
- **The Fasor Engenharia brand appears only inside the generated document, never in the application** — the app is a SaaS MVP for a design partner, not Fasor's tool. The brand is edited in exactly one place, **Registries › Empresa**, and drawn inside the app only in that tab's Brand preview.
- **Amber (not red) for "out of limit"** and for every "proposed, not confirmed" value. Rationale: the app highlights and suggests, the engineer decides; red is reserved for what the engineer declared non-conforming. One amber convention covers plate fields, display readings, dictation, captions, the conclusion line, the generated text and the suggested deadline. "Verificar" (dashed border) marks an ungrounded guess as a second level of trust.
- **Violet `nao-ensaiado`** for equipment that exists but was not tested (today these vanish into prose in section 8) and for sync conflicts — visible without reading as pass or fail.
- **NA is neutral on purpose**, not a lesser "conforme". *Com restrições* uses amber because a restriction is a flag, not a failure.
- **Typography: one family, Inter** (fallback Roboto → Segoe UI → system-ui), for large x-height and unambiguous digits. Numerals always `tabular-nums` so columns of readings align; measured values in `value` 20px/600 because the reading is what the engineer checks against the limit. The ramp is deliberately larger than a desktop default (body 16 / field-input 18 / value 20 / heading 18 / title 22 / display 28); **nothing below 14px**. **No ALL-CAPS labels** — the current Excel sheets truncate uppercase labels ("TENSÃO SECUNDÁ", "CAPACIDADE INTERRU"); labels are sentence case and wrap rather than clip. Units live in a separate slot beside the value ("330" + "MΩ"), never inside the number string, so the unit can be a control.
- **Focus is the whole field** (`focus-fill` background + 3px `focus` border), lifted from the GroundPRO screenshots: with gloves and glare the engineer must know without doubt which field is live. Non-input elements take a 3px ring with 2px offset on the full hit area.
- **Depth is drawn with borders, not shadows** (a soft shadow disappears under sunlight; a 1–2px border does not). Shadows only on the Photo capture sheet and dialogs, always over the scrim. Elevation is never hierarchy.
- **Density:** single-column equipment sheet at every width (one field per row, unit beside it); content column capped at 880px; desktop spends extra width on a persistent tree, not on wider forms; measurement value cells 200px at tablet/desktop (wide enough for "3.700" + a unit control, narrow enough for three readings per row on a 768px tablet). No dense desktop-style tables on field surfaces.
- **Shapes:** 4px for inputs and table cells (they should look like a form, not a chat bubble); 8px for buttons, block cards, segmented controls, photo tiles; 12px for Laudo cards, dialogs and the capture sheet; full round only on status pills, sync badge, not-tested chip and photo number badges.
- **Disabled state never signals by opacity alone:** 40% opacity plus a mandatory reason in `label` / `ink-secondary` beside the control, which stays focusable.
- **The letter, not the fill, carries state:** tri-state letters are set in `heading` 18px/600; the selected fill is secondary.
- **Camera or copy before keyboard:** an empty group opens with the "Fotografar" tile or a copy chip, "Digitar" as a text link — never the keyboard as the first affordance.
- **Key palette values.** Light: surface-base #F4F5F7, surface-raised #FFFFFF, surface-sunken #E6E9ED, ink-primary #15181D, ink-secondary #454B54, border-strong #5C6470, border-hairline #C9CED6, primary #1F4E79, focus-fill #DCE8F5, conforme #1B6B36 / #DDF3E4, nao-conforme #B42318 / #FBE3E0, nao-aplica #5C6470 / #E6E9ED, fora-do-limite #8A4B00 / #FDEBD0, nao-ensaiado #4A4374 / #E9E6F5, toast-action #86B6E8, scrim rgba(21,24,29,0.45). Dark pairs exist for all 27 light tokens. Spacing scale 4/8/12/16/24/32/48, touch-min 48, touch-field 56, gutters 16/24/32, content-max 880, breakpoints 768 / 1280, rail-width 320, thumb-inline 64, thumb-grid 96.

## 7. Accessibility requirements (concrete and testable)

Floor: **WCAG 2.2 AA across all surfaces**; ink on surfaces targets **7:1** because of sunlight, not only compliance; semantic letters on their fills are ≥ 4.5:1 and carry the state with their weight.

Measured and verified (recomputed by the accessibility review with a WCAG relative-luminance script; every ratio claimed in DESIGN.md is numerically correct):
- ink-primary on surface-raised 17.79:1 (light) / 14.85:1 (dark); on surface-base 16.31:1 / 16.77:1.
- ink-secondary on surface-raised 8.80:1; on surface-base 8.06:1; on focus-fill 7.08:1.
- primary on surface-raised 8.66:1, on surface-base 7.94:1; primary-dark on raised-dark 7.68:1 / base-dark 8.67:1.
- White on primary 8.66:1; dark foreground on primary-dark 8.18:1; ink on focus-fill 14.32:1.
- conforme 6.56:1 white / 5.62:1 on fill; nao-conforme 6.57 / 5.37; nao-aplica 5.98 / 4.91 (thinnest margin); fora-do-limite 6.80 / 5.82; nao-ensaiado 8.95 / 7.29. Dark-theme equivalents 6.2–7.8:1 and above.

Behavioral requirements:
- **Hit areas** ≥ 48px (`touch-min`) for anything tappable regardless of glyph size; ≥ 56px (`touch-field`) for field controls. Enumerated cases: drag handles, overflow triggers, tree chevrons (48×56 on the left of the row), Sync badge, App bar back and avatar, unit control (≥ 48×56, separated by a 2px `border-strong` divider), quick chips, text buttons, toast actions, the "Tap TAG" affordance, the rail toggle, palette rows (the whole 56px row is the target, the "+" is decorative).

- **Screen-reader announcements**: Tri-state announces "Item 8, Contatos, Conforme, 1 de 3"; Measurement field announces value, unit and limit state ("330 megaohms, abaixo do aceitável"), the unit slot carries the spoken unit as its label (megaohms, gigaohms, microohms, quilovolts); dates are `<time>` elements with the full date; the out-of-limit helper is announced once on blur via `role="status"`; calculated cells announce "calculado"; the Quantity stepper announces "Seccionadoras, 25"; moves announce "SEC-C09 movido para a posição 3 de 5"; the filter announces "Mostrando 4 fotos do Cubículo Enel"; the Suggestion field announces "Sugerido, 147 gigaohms, confirmar"; the Generated text field announces "Texto da conclusão, sugerido, ⟨texto⟩, confirmar".

- **Focus visibility and obstruction** (SC 2.4.11 / 2.4.13): non-input focus = 3px `focus` ring with 2px offset on the full hit area; the focused element is never hidden under the Sticky action bar or the keyboard.
- **Generated text is auditable without sight**: the Criteria line is the `aria-describedby` of its Generated text field; the Photo stamp is the tile's time description.
