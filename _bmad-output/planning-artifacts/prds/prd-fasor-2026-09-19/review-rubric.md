# PRD Quality Review — Releng (prd-fasor-2026-09-19)

## Overall verdict

This is a substantive PRD, not furniture: the thesis is stated and bet on, the journeys are earned, the evidence is graded honestly (§10, §11), and the Non-Goals do real work. What is at risk is downstream usability and done-ness. The document contradicts itself on the one thing it treats as a principle — how many pre-issue conditions block an export — it omits seven FRs including the entire Template composer from §7.1 In Scope, it silently drops the unconfirmed conclusion text out of both the printed document and the pre-issue list, and it demotes the technical risk the addendum calls "the single largest technical unknown in the product" to a `[NOTE FOR PM]` under the wrong FR while guaranteeing zero photo loss on that same platform. None of these needs new thinking; all of them need a decision written down. Fix the five high findings and this PRD can go to epics and stories.

## Decision-readiness — adequate

The PRD states decisions as decisions. §7.2's cut order names what goes first and what never goes. §9 keeps a WCAG failure it chose to keep ("disabled controls are rendered at an opacity computed at 2.04:1, kept against the accessibility review's recommendation") instead of smoothing it. §11 Evidence is unusually candid — "5 claims verified against 23 unverified, 2 disputed and 2 refuted" and "no competitor product was ever used with a login". §10 grades its own regulatory reading as "Firm, verified" against "Uncertain, and treated as such". A reader pushing back on the competitive framing finds their objection already in Q16.

Where it weakens is §13. The section claims to be "Ordered by how much they can change what gets built" and then is not. Q11 (field ergonomics, which the PRD itself says the spines flag "as the assumption to validate **before the sheet layout is built**"), Q12 (the LGPD basis for sending client photographs to an AI provider, which gates all of §5.5) and Q13 (location depth, which the PRD states is already known to be wrong — "It does not, in at least two known cabines") all rank below Q5, whose own text says "Affects FR-14 only". Two of the seventeen are not open at all: Q14 and Q15 are answered elsewhere in this document.

The larger gap is what §13 does not contain. Neither iPadOS Safari storage eviction nor the AI unit-cost-per-reading appears as an open question, though both can change what is built more than any of Q2 through Q9.

### Findings

- **high** iPadOS storage eviction is the biggest build risk and is not treated as one (§13, FR-56, FR-57, §12) — The addendum calls it "the single largest technical unknown in the product: **photo-safe offline capture must be proven before anything else is built on it.**" In the PRD it appears once, as a `[NOTE FOR PM]` appended to FR-57 (storage pressure), framed as "architecture's call". Meanwhile §12 mandates iPadOS Safari as a supported platform, FR-56 promises "No captured photo is lost under **any** sequence", §9 sizes for three days offline, and SM-6 targets zero photos lost on the real job. There is no open question, no spike, and no fallback named. *Fix:* add a §13 question above Q1 — "can a browser on iPadOS hold three days of photos safely, and if not, is home-screen installation a shipping precondition?" — and move the eviction note from FR-57 to FR-54/FR-56 where the guarantee lives.
- **high** §13's stated ordering is false, and three build-blockers sit below single-FR questions (§13) — Q11 blocks the sheet layout, Q12 is legal input gating the §5.5 differentiator, Q13 is already known to be wrong; all rank below Q5 ("Affects FR-14 only"), Q8 and Q9. *Fix:* re-rank Q11, Q12 and Q13 into positions 2–4, or delete the ordering claim rather than assert an order the list does not have.
- **medium** Q14 is a decision the PRD already made (§13 Q14 against §6, FR-6) — "Who reviews in the office? No reviewer role and no review surface are specified" is answered by §6 ("no roles, no reviewer surface") and FR-6 ("There are no roles: every user of the company sees every Project and Laudo"). The genuinely open item is narrower and sharper: FR-21 defines a status *Em revisão* that no FR produces, consumes or exits. *Fix:* rewrite as "FR-21 defines a status with no FR behind it — delete *Em revisão* or define what enters and leaves it", and merge into Q2.
- **medium** Q15 is also already decided (§13 Q15 against §6) — §6 puts "any load or demand study" in the data-model slot regardless of the answer; the PRD's behavior does not change either way. *Fix:* close it, or restate it as a naming question for a future type and drop it to the bottom.
- **low** Q1 disables the measurement occasion for four metrics and §8 does not say so (§13 Q1, §8) — SM-1, SM-3, SM-5 and SM-6 are all "measured on Fasor's next real cabine primária job", which Q1 says does not exist. *Fix:* one line in §8 stating that every primary target is unmeasurable until Q1 resolves.

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The thesis is explicit and falsifiable: competitors already do offline capture, so "sync reliability has become a qualifying threshold rather than a selling point", and the wager is on the provider's own document plus the least typing in the category. Feature order follows it — §5.9 generation is the feature that ships regardless, the assists come next by user mandate, and the cut order protects exactly the two assists that carry the keystroke claim. MVP scope kind is coherent (problem-solving, one report type done properly). §5.8's own description refuses to claim credit for offline ("Getting this right does not win the sale; getting it wrong eliminates the product") — that is thesis discipline.

The gap is between the thesis and §8. Half the wager is unmetered and the regulatory wedge is entirely unmetered.

- **high** 55 of 74 FRs are validated by no Success Metric, and the omitted set includes the whole action plan (§8 against §5.7) — FR-49 to FR-53 are named in §2 as the NR-10 10.7.11 wedge and in addendum §5 as defensibility gap 2 ("points of attention with photo, corrective action, deadline and owner **on the cabine**"), and no SM touches them. - **medium** The cut order is untraced to journeys and metrics (§7.2) — cutting FR-38 removes Flow 3's climax entirely; cutting FR-39 removes Flow 4's climax and one of SM-3's five named validators. The cut order says only which FRs go. *Fix:* name the journey and metric consequence beside each cut.

## Done-ness clarity — thin

Most FRs carry genuinely testable consequences, and several are exemplary: FR-11's "**Checklist items are never removed by a subtype** — only pre-marked", FR-26's "sets only unset rows; NC rows and NA defaults are untouched", FR-41's "Nothing unconfirmed is written, counted by the progress indicator, or printed", FR-64's "**This is the only place the export revision prints**". An engineer can build from those.

But the section that matters most for correctness — what happens at export — contradicts itself, and three FRs are not implementable as written. This is the dimension story creation leans on hardest, and it is the weakest one here.

### Findings

- **high** FR-1 creates a second blocking condition that FR-73, §11 and SM-C4 all say does not exist (FR-1 against FR-73) — FR-1: "Razão social and logo are **required before the first export**; the pre-issue check names whichever is missing." FR-73: missing company brand data is in the list, and "**Only one row blocks generation: an unset parecer**"; "Everything else warns." §11 repeats "One blocking pre-issue item, and only one", and SM-C4 makes it a budget. An implementer must pick one and the PRD does not say which. *Fix:* make FR-1 read "warned in the pre-issue list, never blocking" and say what the document prints with no logo — or amend FR-73, §11 and SM-C4 to two blockers and justify the second.
- **high** An unconfirmed conclusion text disappears from the document with no warning (FR-30, FR-68, FR-73) — FR-30: "An unconfirmed text does not block concluding the sheet and does not print; the conclusion row then prints the result pair alone." FR-68 prints "its confirmed conclusion text". FR-73's minimum list covers unconfirmed *readings* and uncaptioned *photos* but not unconfirmed conclusion text. A sheet can therefore be *Concluída*, pass every pre-issue check and print without the paragraph — which is precisely the failure mode §5.10 says the list exists to prevent ("issuing a report with a blank checklist column, as the real source document did across all 94 sheets"). *Fix:* add "sheets concluded with unconfirmed conclusion text" to FR-73's list.
- **high** FR-68 defines the *Agrupar por tipo* order for only 6 of the 8 equipment types (FR-68 against FR-22) — the fixed order given is "Seccionadoras, Disjuntores, TP and TC as per-column pairs, then Transformadores with their feeder cables". Para-raio and Cabos de entrada have no position. EXPERIENCE.md carries this as an open assumption ("where para-raios and cabos de entrada/saída print inside a cabine with *Agrupar por tipo* on (no example in the current document)"); the PRD drops it without inheriting the question. *Fix:* state their position in FR-68, or add it to §13 and mark FR-68 incomplete until answered.
- **high** FR-55 is unbounded and contradicts itself in two bullets (FR-55) — "A Laudo is downloaded to a signed-in device without the user asking" names no trigger and no set: every Laudo? every Laudo in every Project? all history? Then the second consequence says "A Laudo not on this device is shown as unavailable", which cannot happen if all are downloaded. The bound ("how many active laudos one device holds") is exiled to addendum §1, on a device that FR-57 says runs out of storage. *Fix:* state the rule in the FR — e.g. Laudos in status *Em campo* or *Rascunho* for this company, most recent N, downloaded over Wi-Fi only.
- **medium** FR-61 is a duplicate of FR-32 and its one consequence restates the requirement (FR-61 against FR-32) — FR-32 already carries "Reopening the app after a crash or a closed tab offers 'Rascunho encontrado — Recuperar'". FR-61's entire testable content is "Recovery is offered explicitly, never applied silently." *Fix:* fold FR-61 into FR-32, or make it distinct (what screen state is recovered, how long it survives, what happens if the recovered state conflicts with synced data).
- **medium** FR-54's last bullet names scenarios with no expected outcome (FR-54) — "Acceptance tests must cover: closing the app mid-sheet, losing the network mid-upload, and filling the device's storage" is a test plan, not a consequence; it says what to try, not what must be true afterwards. *Fix:* state the outcome for each ("no captured value is lost", "the upload resumes without duplicating", "capture is refused only after a warning that names remaining space").
- **medium** FR-56's headline is unfalsifiable and its capacity bound is competitor-derived (FR-56) — "under **any** sequence of app closure, network failure or storage pressure" cannot be tested to completion; and "A per-laudo photo capacity is supported above the nearest competitor's limit of 300" anchors to a competitor rather than to the workload (the reference job carries about 82). *Fix:* bound the claim to the enumerated sequences plus the addendum's eviction case, and state the capacity as an absolute (e.g. 500 photos per Laudo, 3 concurrent Laudos).
- **medium** §9 Performance is the only NFR with no bound at all (§9) — "Opening a sheet, marking a checklist and capturing a photo are **immediate**", and "Generation time for a 124-page document with about 80 photographs **has no stated tolerance**. `[NOTE FOR PM]` Architecture needs a number." Every other bullet in §9 carries bounds. Architecture cannot negotiate against nothing. *Fix:* state a first-cut budget the architecture may argue with (e.g. sheet open < 300 ms warm, shutter-to-saved < 1 s, generation < 3 minutes for 124 pages).
- **low** FR-46's only consequence restates the requirement (FR-46) — "The composer is reached from an explicit action, never opened automatically." *Fix:* add what the composer must do (chips populate from this Laudo's recent activities and locations; edits save without a Save action; a caption edit after export marks numbering provisional per FR-48).
