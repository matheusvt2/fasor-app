# fasor — Experience Spine

> The UI shows the literal placeholder **PRODUTO** where the product name would go (rule and rationale: `DESIGN.md` § Brand & Style; decisions are logged in `.memlog.md`, cited as "memlog"). Personas, scope and requirements are inherited by reference from the sources above and not restated. UI copy is Portuguese (Brazil); this document is English with pt-BR microcopy inline. `DESIGN.md` owns how it looks; token references use the brace-path syntax (for example `{colors.primary}`) and resolve to its frontmatter.

## Foundation

- **Form-factor:** responsive web app, **tablet-first**, phone-capable, desktop for office composition and review (memlog; brief). No native apps, no app-store publishing.
- **Type as little as possible: the app captures by camera and infers; the engineer confirms** (brief § The Solution; user mandate, memlog). **Interaction budget:** a fully conforme seccionadora with a copied nameplate must be completable in **≤ 20 taps and ≤ 15 keystrokes with signal**, the readings coming from "Ler visor"; **without signal the nine readings are typed, about 36 keystrokes**, because the engineer needs each value on the spot (decision 2026-09-21). The budget is a claim about connectivity, not about the interface, and is reported that way (§ Smart Input › Interaction budget). Any field not fillable by tap, photo, copy or default is an exception this spine must justify (§ Smart Input). All AI runs in the backend (SaaS, memlog): offline, the photo is stored and queued; the reading runs at the first connection; results arrive as amber suggestions with the source crop beside the value, and nothing is written until the engineer taps.
- **Simplicity and ease of use above completeness: every field, screen and step must justify its cost to the engineer** (user decision, 2026-09-18, memlog). Applied to the reference-tool update of the same day: the priority fills the deadline instead of adding a field; the council choice is one tap prefilled from the profile; the document control table is composed from data already entered, never typed; the generated conclusion text is one confirming tap; the company profile is a once-per-company tab with two required fields (razão social, logo) and everything else optional; the photo stamp costs nothing at capture. No emoji anywhere in the product (icons come from the SVG sprite).
- **UI system: TBD by architecture.** `DESIGN.md` tokens are self-contained (hex, font stacks, px) and must be mapped onto whatever system architecture picks. This spine specifies full behavior, not a delta.
- **Two contexts, one app.** *Office* (any computer, connected — nothing to install, it is a web page): compose Templates, set up the Relatório, review, export, **and finish or correct anything captured in the field**. *Field* (tablet/phone, usually no signal): fill sheets, photograph, add or remove the odd block. Composing is the office rule and the field exception; filling happens mostly in the field, **but nothing captured is ever locked to the field** (decision 2026-09-21): every sheet, reading, photo, caption and point of attention stays editable after the visit, from any computer, and photos can be added during the visit or at any time after it. Editing after an export simply makes the next revision (Capture-to-Document › Export revisions).
- **One device fills a relatório at a time** (decision 2026-09-21 — parallel filling is not needed). A second person's photos come in by import, during or after the visit (§ Smart Input › Photos); no second device ever edits sheets in the MVP, so there is nothing to merge.
- **Field context [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions]:** touch with leather gloves (luva de vaqueta) possible; frequent one-handed use; variable light (dark indoor cubicles, direct sun outdoors); zero signal inside the substation; one device per relatório, with a possible second executor on a different sheet from another device.
- **Identity:** sign-in required, simple (memlog). Named users (Bruno, Eduardo); every sheet records who filled it and when.
- **Access:** all users of the company see all projects and relatórios; no roles, no per-relatório sharing in the MVP (memlog). One user per device session; users are provisioned outside the app for the MVP (Open Question).
- **Theme:** follows the device's system preference, with a manual override in Account (Sistema / Claro / Escuro) — memlog; see `DESIGN.md` § Brand & Style.
- **Stakes:** B2B internal-grade MVP for one design partner, on a data model ready for SaaS. Regulated content (NR-10, PIE) lives in the *output*, not in the app's own compliance posture.

→ Visual references and the conflict rule: `DESIGN.md` § Brand & Style.

## Block Model

The organizing principle (memlog, verbatim from the user): the relatório is *"como se fosse um draw.io com objetos prontos em que o usuário pode adicionar ou remover"*.

| Object | Definition | Where edited |
|---|---|---|
| **Template** | A reusable, ordered composition of blocks with quantities and sub-block defaults, **carrying the location skeleton** — cabines and columns, with the equipment quantities per column — so that every block of a new relatório is born in its place with its final suggested TAG (memlog). Not tied to a project. | Template composer (office) |
| **Relatório** | An instance of a Template inside a Project, for one visit. Owns the filled data, photos, points of attention, export history. | Relatório setup, Relatório overview, Equipment sheet |
| **Block (coarse)** | A whole section or a whole equipment sheet. Addable, removable, reorderable. Two kinds: **Section block** (FO.SERV-03 sections 1–6, 8, 10, 11 — mostly fixed text with a few variables; the boilerplate is authored in the Template composer and **can also be edited inside one relatório** (decision 2026-09-21 — freedom to change the report's objects): the edit stays in that relatório and never flows back to the template, so changing the objective for Porto Seguro does not change the next client's; office surface only, never a field one; plain text with variables in the MVP, rich text later (FR-12)) and **Equipment block** (one sheet, one piece of equipment, one TAG — mandatory at creation, suggested automatically; see Registries › Equipment identity). | Block palette › Relatório overview / Template composer |
| **Sub-block (fine)** | A part inside a block: a test (isolação, resistência de contato, relação de transformação), a checklist item, a nameplate group, the observation box. **Ownership:** the Template defines the defaults per block type/subtype; a Relatório may override per sheet (Block palette opened from inside that sheet). A sub-block toggled off is omitted from that sheet's print; it is never printed empty. Checklist items are never removed: subtype NA defaults premark rows and remain editable. | Block palette opened from inside a sheet; Template composer |
| **Location** | Cabine › Coluna/Cubículo (a cabine may itself be named "Cubículo", as Cubículo Enel is; the second level is always the column). Equipment blocks hang off a location. **The Cabine owns** its substation characteristics (type, voltages, power) and its test environment (temperature, humidity, altitude) — each cabine in the document has its own — plus the flag *Agrupar por tipo* used by section 9 (Capture-to-Document). **One editing place:** the block labeled **"Da cabine"** on the cabine's first sheet (office or field; the edit lands on the cabine, and every other sheet in it shows the values read-only under the same label). The cabine row in Relatório overview shows them read-only in its `meta` line; its Overflow menu keeps *Agrupar por tipo* and "Abrir primeira ficha". Printed once per cabine, on its first sheet, whatever the section 9 order. Locations come from the Template skeleton; adding or renaming a cabine or column in a relatório is done from the tree (office rule, field exception). | Equipment sheet ("Da cabine" block on the first sheet); Relatório overview (tree — flag and structure only) |

**The 8 equipment block types** (from the 8 sheet models in `imports/extract-fo-serv-03.md`; the brief counts 6 because it groups cables and TP/TC):

| Block type (pt-BR label) | Nameplate sub-block | Checklist | Test sub-blocks | Conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cabos de entrada | — (as the first sheet of a cabine it also shows the cabine's Características da SE + Ambiente de ensaio) | 5 items | Isolação (Fase A/B/C/Reserva × Massa) | Yes |
| Para-raio (entrada / saída) | 6 fields | 5 items | Isolação (A/B/C/Reserva) | Yes |
| Chave seccionadora | 9 fields | 14 items | Isolação contato aberto (T1/T2, T3/T4, T5/T6) + contato fechado (A/B/C) · Resistência de contato (T1-T2, T3-T4, T5-T6) | Yes |
| Disjuntor MT | 12 fields (including AJ. RELÉ 50/51) | 15 items | Same as seccionadora | Yes |
| TP — proteção | 12 fields | 15 items | Isolação (R/S/T) · Relação de transformação (H1-H2 / X1-X2, three phases) | Yes |
| TC — proteção | 12 fields (Relação, Exatidão) | 15 items | Isolação (R/S/T) · Relação (P1-P2 / S1-S2) | Yes |
| Cabos de saída / alimentação TRn | — | 5 items | Isolação (A/B/C/Reserva) | Yes |
| Transformador de força | 12 fields | 15 items | Isolação (3 combinations Primário/Secundário/Massa) · Relação por TAP (H1-H3/X1-X0 …) | **Yes — restored** (missing today on all 8 transformer sheets) |

**Acceptance criteria belong to the test sub-block type, not to the instrument** — the canonical statement (operator + value + unit + source, seeding, per-sheet override, what the Instrument supplies) is Registries › Acceptance criteria.

Every equipment block also carries: **state** (Vazia · Em preenchimento · Concluída · Não ensaiada + motivo), **filled by / when**, **photos**, **observations**. Subtype defaults (a seco / a óleo; manual / motorizada) may premark items NA; the rows stay editable [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions]. State names are feminine in tree rows ("Não ensaiada" — a ficha) and masculine on the chip ("Não ensaiado" — o equipamento); this agreement is intentional.

Rules: a Template can be edited without touching relatórios already created from it. A Relatório remembers which Template it came from. **Tree order is per relatório**: the Template only sets the initial order; the engineer reorders freely and the order is stored with the relatório. Removing a block from a Relatório that already has data asks for confirmation and keeps the data recoverable until export ("Restaurar ficha removida" in the Relatório overview Overflow menu). "Duplicar" copies a block's structure (sub-blocks, subtype), not its data, and asks for a new TAG.

## Smart Input

The principle is the Foundation's second bullet and the brief's (§ The Solution): the app captures by camera and infers; the engineer confirms. Scope is the brief's and is not restated here; this section maps every field group of a sheet to its non-typing path, its fallback and how the value gets confirmed, and states the one pattern they all share.

| Field group | Non-typing path | Fallback | Confirmation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nameplate** (6–12 fields per type) | Camera first: an empty nameplate group opens with a full-width "Fotografar placa" tile (Camera capture button, single shot); the photo is saved as a sheet photo (caption "placa de identificação"), queued, and read by LLM vision in the backend into one Suggestion field per nameplate field. Above the fields, two one-tap actions when they apply: **"Copiar da última visita (SEC-ENT)"** (the TAG existed in a previous relatório of the same site) and **"Igual à SEC-C04?"** (another sheet of the same type and model in this relatório already has a plate). | "Digitar" as a text link under the tile; typing into a field discards that field's suggestion. | Per field, or "Confirmar todos" (provenance rule below). Registry cross-check: the voltage class must exist in Registries; an unknown manufacturer asks "Criar Celtta?" inline. |
| **Measurement readings** | **Typing is the primary path, because the engineer decides on the spot** (decision 2026-09-21): he needs the number in front of the equipment to judge it against the criterion and to re-test when it is low. A unit suffix keeps it short ("147G", "3.7T", "330M"); Enter moves down the column and on into the next table, so the sheet's readings are one continuous run; the unit defaults from the previous row. **"Ler visor"** (Read display button, post-MVP — FR-36) is an assist, never a gate: **with signal**, a photo of the display returns value + unit into the next empty cell(s) as Suggestion fields within seconds, burst mode row by row; **without signal**, it never leaves a cell empty — the engineer types the value and the queued photo becomes its evidence: when the reading runs, it **checks** the typed value and never overwrites it (match → the crop attaches silently; mismatch → amber "Visor: 147 GΩ · digitado 14,7 GΩ — Conferir"). **Dictation** (post-MVP — FR-40): "Fase A, 147 giga" → a Suggestion field. | — (typing is the path) | Typed values are the engineer's own and need no confirmation. An OCR suggestion is confirmed per cell (Enter) or "Confirmar todos" per table; an OCR mismatch is resolved by one tap on the value to keep. |
| **Units** | Read from the display by OCR; parsed from the typed suffix; otherwise defaulted from the previous row of the same table. | The unit toggle: a tap cycles MΩ → GΩ → TΩ (no mini-Combobox). | **Magnitude-outlier warning** within a table, amber helper, never a block: "Fase C 1000× abaixo de A e B. Conferir?". |
| **Checklist** (5–15 rows) | **Never pre-marked Conforme silently.** The Bulk action bar at the head of the list, mirrored in the Sticky action bar while the checklist is on screen, offers **"Marcar os restantes como Conforme"** (sets only unset rows; NC rows and NA defaults untouched) and **"Repetir da ficha anterior do mesmo tipo"** (copies the C/NC/NA pattern, not observations). Later, not MVP: vision over the sheet's photos proposes NC rows as suggestions (Open Questions). | Tri-state control per row. | The bulk tap is the confirmation; it is undoable ("11 itens marcados Conforme — Desfazer" Toast). |
| **NC observation** | Dictation button on the Observation field; **per-item chips** (item 8 Contatos → "sinais de aquecimento" · "oxidação" · "desgaste"); when the NC row has a photo, LLM vision drafts one sentence on sync ("Contatos fixos com sinais de aquecimento") as a suggestion. | Typing. | Chips insert plain text; dictation and the vision draft arrive as a Suggestion field to confirm or edit. |
| **Conclusion** | An amber suggestion under the Conclusion control, computed from the sheet: every row C or NA and every reading within limit → **"Aprovado · Sem restrições?"**; any NC row or out-of-limit reading → **"Aprovado · Com restrições?"**. **Reprovado is never suggested.** | The two radiogroups. | One tap applies both pairs; nothing is set until tapped. |

**Provenance-and-confirm pattern** — shared by every assist above. A value the engineer did not enter is rendered by the **Suggestion field**: `{colors.fora-do-limite-fill}` background, a **"Sugerido"** pill and, for OCR and vision, the **source crop** as a thumbnail beside the value (tap to zoom the region on the photo). **"Confirmar"** per field. **"Confirmar todos"** confirms every "Sugerido" field of the group and **skips every field flagged "Verificar"** — an ungrounded or low-confidence guess, shown with its best guess rather than blank (an editable guess costs a glance, a blank costs typing) and confirmable only by a tap on that field itself. **Capture shape vs. print shape of the insulation test.** The form's Bloco C — the plain *ENSAIO DE ISOLAÇÃO* on the cabos, para-raio, TP, TC and transformador sheets — prints five value columns: `VALORES {30 SEGUNDOS, 1 MINUTO, ESTAB./10MIN}` and `QUALIDADE ISOLAÇÃO {ABSORÇÃO, POLARIZAÇÃO}`. **The MVP captures one of them.** The rule, so that nobody has to infer it:

- The app's single value column on those tables is **`1 minuto`**, labelled with that name and not "Valor". It is the only timing Fasor measures today — in the delivered FO.SERV-03 every other column of every Bloco C table is "-".
- At generation the full five-column grid prints. The captured value lands in `1 MINUTO`; `30 SEGUNDOS`, `ESTAB./10MIN`, `ABSORÇÃO` and `POLARIZAÇÃO` print "-". The document is then byte-for-byte the shape Bruno signs today, and the "sub-blocks toggled off are omitted, never printed empty" rule does **not** apply to these four columns: they are part of the grid, not sub-blocks.
- The **contato aberto / contato fechado** variant (seccionadora, disjuntor) genuinely has a single VALORES column in the form and carries no timing qualifier; its app column stays **`Valor`**. Same for resistência ôhmica de contato (µΩ). Two shapes, two labels, no third.
- Absorção and polarização **are not calculated by the app** (decision 2026-09-21, after research): the megôhmetro Fasor uses, the Instrum DMG10Ki, computes the índice de absorção and the índice de polarização itself, as do the Megabras and Fluke 10 kV testers. They print "-" by default. A template can turn on an optional pair of columns, **"IA e IP lidos do visor"** — two numbers typed from the instrument's display, never three timings divided by the app — and then they print in `ABSORÇÃO` and `POLARIZAÇÃO`, with `30 SEGUNDOS` and `ESTAB./10MIN` still "-". The toggle is a sub-block default in the Template composer and is **off in the Fasor template**, so Bruno never sees the columns. Importing readings straight from the instrument over Bluetooth (Fluke Connect, Megger CertSuite, Megabras BlueLogg) is the best format in the market and the long-term direction; it stays out of the MVP (brief).

- *Cabines in tree order.* Each cabine has the flag *Agrupar por tipo* (editable from its row in Relatório overview; the seed template sets it as in the current document — off for Cubículo Enel, Oxigênio, Cobertura A and Cobertura B; on for 1° Subsolo and Geradores).

- *Flag on →* the cabine yields one subsection per type group, in the fixed order Seccionadoras › Disjuntores › TP's e TC's (printed as TP + TC pairs per column) › Transformadores e cabos de alimentação (printed as Cabos de alimentação TRn + Transformador TRn pairs); a group with no sheets is skipped; within a group, sheets follow tree order (column by column). Para-raios and cabos de entrada/saída only occur in ungrouped cabines in the current document; in a grouped cabine they print inside the seccionadoras group, in tree order [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions].

- *Ordem de campo* — **the base case, kept, not exposed.** Walk the tree: one subsection per cabine, sheets in tree order. "Por local e tipo" is built on top of it, so it is not a second implementation but the floor the first one stands on. Keep it reachable outside the UI (a flag, or the fallback when grouping cannot resolve), because the grouping rests on an assumption this spine flags as unconfirmed — where para-raios and cabos de entrada/saída print inside a grouped cabine (Open Questions). If that assumption is wrong on the first real job, the base case still produces a usable document; without it there is no output until the grouping is fixed.

**Export revisions.** One export = one revision = the DOCX and the PDF of the same content, produced together. **A draft is not a revision** (decision 2026-09-21): "Pré-visualizar" runs the same generator with a RASCUNHO watermark and without a revision number or a status change, so the engineer can read the whole report as the client will before issuing anything; the draft and the issued document must be identical except for the watermark and the revision line (one test guards it). ## Interaction Primitives

- **Tap to act; one primary action per surface.** No hover-only affordances (touch first).
- **Autosave** on every field change (debounced — value is architecture's call; the UX only requires that leaving a field never loses it) to local storage, then to the server when online. No Save button on sheets; "Concluir ficha" marks state, it does not save. A visually hidden "Salvo" status is announced at most every few seconds, never per keystroke.
- **Reorder three ways, always:** press and hold 300 ms + drag; Overflow menu "Subir · Descer" (and "Mover para…" where a location can change); keyboard Alt+↑/↓ wherever the element is focusable and a keyboard is attached. The new position is announced ("SEC-C09 movido para a posição 3 de 5"). No swipe gestures (gloves, glare) except system back.
- **Enter moves down a measurement column; Tab moves right.** Numeric fields open the decimal keypad (`inputmode="decimal"`).
- **Next/previous sheet** follows tree order; "Próxima coluna" jumps locations.
- **Camera or copy before keyboard.** Every field group has a non-typing path (§ Smart Input); the keyboard is the fallback, reached by a text link, never the first affordance of an empty group.
- **Photos:** the Camera capture button in the Sticky action bar opens the device camera directly; burst until "Concluir fotos"; caption defaults silently from context. Numbering is never manual.
- **Bulk over per-row, never silent.** "Marcar os restantes como Conforme" and "Repetir da ficha anterior" are one tap with undo; no row is pre-marked Conforme by the app.
- **Suggestions are never writes.** Out-of-limit hints, OCR and vision readings, dictation results, the vision caption and the conclusion suggestion are shown in amber and applied only by an explicit tap; "Confirmar todos" never includes a "Verificar" field; the source crop stays beside an OCR'd value until export.
- **Undo** for destructive-but-recoverable actions via a persistent Toast action ("Ficha removida — Desfazer") that stays until dismissed or until the next navigation, at least 20 s; after that, "Restaurar ficha removida" in the Relatório overview Overflow menu until export.
- **Banned:** carousels, infinite scroll (registries are paginated — page size is architecture's call), auto-decisions on verdicts, pre-marked Conforme, AI values written without a tap, a modal choice before the camera, modal stacks > 1, color-only state, tooltips as the only explanation, clearing a selection by re-tapping it, explanatory lines repeated on every sheet.

## Accessibility Floor

Behavioral; contrast lives in `DESIGN.md`.

- WCAG 2.2 AA across all surfaces; ink on surfaces targets 7:1 because of sunlight, not only compliance; semantic letters on their fills are ≥ 4.5:1 and carry the state with their weight (`DESIGN.md` § Colors).
- Every control has a hit area ≥ `{spacing.touch-min}` even when its glyph is smaller; field controls ≥ `{spacing.touch-field}`. Reachable one-handed on a tablet in portrait: the primary action sits in the Sticky action bar, which fills the width. Reorder never requires dragging (Interaction Primitives).
- Full keyboard operation wherever a keyboard is attached: Tab order = reading order; Esc closes the top modal; tri-state and conclusion segments are `radiogroup`s with full-word accessible names (arrow keys move, Delete/Backspace clears); tree rows expand with Left/Right.
- **Dialogs and overlays** — Confirm dialog, Export dialog, Photo capture sheet, Block palette, Sync status, Photo viewer, Conflict view — are `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="true"`, labeled by their title (`aria-labelledby`) and described by their sentence (`aria-describedby`); focus is trapped inside; initial focus lands on the non-destructive action ("Cancelar") or the first option; Esc and the system back close them; focus returns to the element that opened them; the background is inert.
- **Errors and helpers are associated**, not just adjacent: every helper, reason or error line is `aria-describedby` of its control; required-missing sets `aria-invalid="true"`; the Login error is additionally `role="alert"`.
- Screen reader: Tri-state control announces "Item 8, Contatos, Conforme, 1 de 3"; Measurement field announces value, unit and limit state ("330 megaohms, abaixo do aceitável") — the unit slot carries the spoken unit as its label (megaohms, gigaohms, microohms, quilovolts) and dates are `<time>` elements with the full date; the out-of-limit helper is announced once on blur via `role="status"`. **Live regions announce transitions, not counts**: the Sync badge announces only state changes (ok ↔ pending ↔ offline ↔ error ↔ conflict); the Sheet header timestamp and the Progress counter are not live, "Completa" is announced once; Toasts are `role="status"`, conflict and sync-error Banners `role="alert"`. State glyphs (✓ ● ○ ⊘, dots) are `aria-hidden`; the word is the accessible text.
- State is never conveyed by color alone: letters C / NC / NA always visible; sync, not-tested, photo-upload and suggestion states carry words ("Sugerido" / "Verificar" pills are text); disabled buttons carry their reason; the priority of a point of attention is always its text ("P0 · Imediata"), the pill's tone is secondary, and the Parecer box carries its verdict word as the title.
- Reordering never requires dragging: the Overflow menu's "Subir · Descer" (and "Mover para…") are the keyboard and glove path, present on every reorderable card; "Adicionar abaixo" places a new block without a drop target.
- Generated text is auditable without sight: the Criteria line is the `aria-describedby` of its Generated text field, so a screen reader hears the values and criteria the paragraph rests on before the confirm action; the Photo stamp is the tile's time description.
- **Smart-input controls:** the Camera capture button and Dictation button have accessible names ("Tirar foto", "Ditar observação"), and dictation announces "Ouvindo…" and then the suggestion text; a Suggestion field announces the pill, the value and the action ("Sugerido, 147 gigaohms, confirmar"), its crop thumbnail has the alt "Recorte do visor" / "Recorte da placa"; the Section stepper is a group of buttons named step + count; the Bulk action bar's undo Toast is `role="status"`. Every assist is reachable without the camera or the microphone (typing remains).
- Text may be enlarged 200% without loss: single-column sheet, tables collapse to stacked rows on narrow widths; at a 320px viewport width, the Sticky action bar unsticks when the viewport is shorter than 480px.
- Reduce motion (`prefers-reduced-motion`): no drag animations, instant toasts, instant drawer/bottom-sheet slides, instant row expansion on NC, no color transition on the out-of-limit change.
- Language: `lang="pt-BR"` on the document; numbers and dates localized.

## Offline & Sync

- **Local first.** Every write lands on the device before anything else; the UI never waits on the network. Photos are kept on the device as captured and never removed locally until the server acknowledges them; compression for upload, and retention of originals after the document is generated, are architecture's call, as long as the original survives until acknowledged.
- **Automatic availability.** Every relatório in *Rascunho* or *Em campo* is downloaded to the device — data, template, registries, photos at thumbnail and full size [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions] — whenever a connection exists, without the user asking (memlog). The Relatório card says so: "No aparelho · atualizado 21:40". A relatório that is not on the device is shown, not hidden, and explains itself when offline (State Patterns › Relatório not on device). *Em revisão* and *Emitido* relatórios are downloaded on open.
- **Visible state always.** Sync badge in the App bar; Sync status lists uploads pending (sheets, photos), **downloads in progress**, last successful sync, errors and conflicts, and — per user — "Último envio de Eduardo: 06/09 18:10". The server cannot know what is still on another device; the last send time is the honest signal and the pre-issue list repeats it.
- **Background sync** when a connection appears (and while the app is open — background execution is not assumed); sheet data first, photos second, so the office sees data early; retry is automatic. Concurrency and resumable upload are architecture's call.
- **Conflict model, per entity.** *Sheet body* (nameplate, checklist, tests, observations, conclusion, not-tested state, photo links of that sheet): **merged by sub-block with fixed rules** (memlog) — a filled value beats an empty one; **NC beats C**; a photo link is never dropped; free text (observations, reasons): the latest edit wins and the other version stays readable in Sync status for the session. Everything merged is listed as information, never asked. Only a **true contradiction** — two different filled values in the same cell (two readings, two conclusions, C vs NA) — opens the Conflict view, with a per-cell pick (the source crop beside an OCR'd value helps decide). *Relatório structure* (block add / move / reorder, locations and cabine data, captions, points of attention, setup): merged automatically — additions from both devices are kept, order is merged by position, the latest edit of a caption or setup field wins and is listed in Sync status. Two cases are surfaced for a decision: **deletion vs. edit** of the same block ("Removido por X, alterado por Y — Manter / Remover") and a **duplicate TAG** created on two devices. Two devices editing different sheets of the same relatório never conflict — this is the intended way for Bruno and Eduardo to split work [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions].
- **Reading queue.** Photos taken for a reading (plate, display, panel) go first in the upload order; the backend reading starts at the first connection while the app is open; results come down with the sheet data as Suggestion fields and are announced once ("3 leituras prontas para confirmar"). The Sync status headline counts "leituras na fila" and "sugestões por confirmar".
- **Export needs connection** (generation is server-side [ASSUMPTION — Open Questions; architecture's call]); the dialog says so when offline, and the pre-issue list shows each colleague's last send time so Bruno can judge whether to wait.
- **Never lose a photo** is the acceptance bar: sync failures in generic field apps are the top complaint in the research (`.working/extract-research.md` §B, §D) and the reason a product gets eliminated, not compared.

→ Mock: `mockups/key-sync-status.html` (queue, downloads, per-user last send, merge rows, the Conflict view — to be reworked for the per-cell pick — and the five badge states), `mockups/key-home.html` (frame 3: offline cold open, relatório not on device).

## Handed to architecture

The UX requires only the behaviors described in this document; the following are architecture's decisions, and any number given in this spine for them is a suggestion, not a requirement:

- **One UX requirement on theming, found by the Playwright sweep (2026-09-21):** whatever mechanism applies the dark theme, every component token that aliases a color (the Suggestion field's amber, the generated-text fill, the P1 priority pill, the parecer box) must resolve **at the element where the theme is set**. The prototype set the theme on an inner element while the aliases resolved on the page root, and every suggestion became light text on a light fill (contrast 1.06). Setting the theme attribute on the root element avoids it.

- Server-side vs. client-side document generation (Offline & Sync; Export dialog).
- The autosave debounce value (Interaction Primitives).
- Registry pagination size and replication to the device (Interaction Primitives; Registries).
- The storage-low threshold and how it is detected (State Patterns › Storage low).
- Photo compression for upload and retention of originals after generation (Offline & Sync).
- Upload concurrency and resumable/chunked upload (Offline & Sync).
- The conflict detection mechanism (base revision vs. version vectors) and where unresolved versions live (Offline & Sync › Conflict model).
- Template snapshot vs. versioned reference for Template → Relatório block definitions — the observable rule is "editing a template never changes existing relatórios" (Block Model).
- The table-of-contents and "Página X de Y" engine and the PDF renderer (Capture-to-Document).
- Session token lifetime and offline re-authentication (State Patterns › Session expired while offline).
- Retention and purge of removed-block data and past export revisions (Block Model; Capture-to-Document › Export revisions).
- The sync trigger and the PWA/service-worker vehicle for the offline shell (Responsive & Platform; State Patterns › App not installed).
- Deduplication of registry entries created offline on two devices (Registries).
- The OCR / vision provider, its cost per reading and the OCR text-layer check that grounds a suggestion (Smart Input); the sub-block merge implementation and how the overridden text version is kept for the session (Offline & Sync › Conflict model).
- The device speech engine used for dictation (browser Web Speech vs. a backend transcription of an audio clip) and its offline pt-BR availability (Dictation button).
- Geolocation-to-altitude at setup (source and precision; the UX needs only "< 1000 m" or a rounded value confirmed once).
- Photo stamp capture: where the coordinates come from (EXIF vs. a geolocation call at capture), the precision printed (four decimals in the UX), and how the watermark and the company logo (PNG/SVG) are rendered in the DOCX as well as the PDF (Capture-to-Document › Header / footer).
- The template engine for the generated conclusion text and the parecer summary (a per-block-type template with the sheet's values, running on the device offline; no model call).
- The maximum offline age before photos must be uploaded, and storage eviction on iPadOS: how long a relatório may sit on a device before photos must be uploaded, and what the user sees if the browser evicted storage meanwhile (Responsive & Platform).
- Inputs the UX still needs answered, phrased as questions: maximum photo resolution/size to keep and whether originals are retained after generation; how many active relatórios a device holds; export time tolerance (how long "Gerando…" may take before it is a failure); timezone/locale for timestamps and photo ordering; user provisioning for the MVP (seeded users, password reset).

## Inspiration & Anti-patterns

- **Lifted from GroundPRO** (`docs/concorrentes/extract-fotos-whatsapp.md` §2–8): Home as a status board with counts; single "Novo relatório" entry; "Rascunho encontrado — Recuperar"; connection-unstable notice; client Combobox "select or type"; whole-field high-contrast focus; numbered section bands on the setup form; example placeholders; acceptance criterion with its source shown where the value is typed; calculated results inline. **Not lifted:** the dark/green theme (reference, not direction — memlog); the tool-card dashboard and the type picker (one report type in the MVP; the type slot exists — see IA › Project); "Importar do dispositivo" file portability (local-first sync replaces a file format; revisit if users ask to move a relatório between accounts). Bruno preferred the older GroundPRO version (reason unknown — Open Question).
- **Rejected — paper as a mode.** Matheus proposed keeping a paper path (photograph the handwritten A4 and let the app read it); Bruno wants to stop filling paper altogether, and the brief puts handwritten-sheet OCR out of the MVP. The app has one capture mode: the sheet on the device.
- **Lifted from Mesh "Teste rápido"** (§9–12): choose equipment type with a quantity stepper before opening sheets (Template composer); required-field counter at the top of a sheet (Progress counter); TAG as the identifier; one field per row with unit in the field; sticky save. **"Usar IA para extrair dados da foto"** is generalized as the **Suggestion field** (Component Patterns, State Patterns, § Smart Input, Flow 2b): camera first on the nameplate, then the same amber-and-confirm pattern for display OCR, captions, NC drafts and equipment identity — nothing is written unconfirmed. The brief's original "last in the queue" was inverted by the user mandate (memlog): the assists ship right after report generation, in the order given in Smart Input › Queue order.
- **Rejected — generic field apps' sync behavior** (`.working/extract-research.md` §D): one photo at a time, "não funciona sem internet", data lost on sync. Offline & Sync above is the answer.
- **Rejected — the current form's defects** (`imports/extract-fo-serv-03.md` §6.5): sheets as pasted images; free-text identification ("CUBÍCULO ENEL" for five pieces of equipment); no executor/date per sheet; manual photo numbering; equipment silently omitted when not tested; instrument data typed 94 times. Each is replaced by a structure in this spine (tree identity, Sheet header, automatic numbering, Not-tested chip, Instrument picker).
- **Lifted from the peer-built load-study tool** (`docs/concorrentes/extract-media-reference-tool.md` §3–5; a reference for the project, not a competitor, per the user on 2026-09-18): numbered editable sections with boilerplate and add-below / move / delete; generated narrative with its formula shown (here the Criteria line); per-company logo, cover, watermark, header/footer (Registries › Empresa); a document control table after the cover; an action plan with P0–P4 priorities and deadlines; photo stamps with date, time and GPS; council-neutral registration (CREA or CRT). **Rejected:** desktop-only, file-fed, mouse-driven use; a local project file ("Salvar projeto / Salvar HTML"; this is a SaaS with local-first sync); the risk matrix and the NR-10 / NBR 5410 adequacy table (not in FO.SERV-03; Open Questions); calibration certificates pasted as images (section 11 already attaches the files); a per-photo value table (the checklist status line is enough). Every lift is tagged [ASSUMPTION — reference tool 2026-09-18] until Bruno reviews.
- **Rejected — auto-verdicts.** The app calculates, compares and highlights; the engineer concludes. Criteria are data with a source (brief); the printed ">400 MΩ" has no known source, so it is shown as "aceitável na ficha", editable, never silently enforced.
