# Architecture-lens Review — fasor

Reviewed: `DESIGN.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `imports/extract-fo-serv-03.md`, `briefs/brief-fasor-2026-09-18/brief.md`. Date: 2026-09-18.

## Overall verdict

The spine is implementable as a product and its Block Model, sheet behavior and registries are 80% specifiable; the seed template plus the extraction give the generator almost everything for sections 0–8, 10, 11. Three things will bite the architecture if built as written: the conflict model covers only sheet bodies while the laudo's structure (tree, blocks, order, photo links, points of attention, setup) can diverge across offline devices; section 9 regrouping and the SE/environment placement cannot be derived from what is written; and the export/revision story (Word-edited DOCX vs. PDF from app data, revision per format, numbering after post-export edits) is unresolved and sits on the critical path of "ready to sign". A fourth, offline-in-a-browser, is under-specified relative to the "never lose a photo" bar.

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical]** Conflict unit is the sheet body only; laudo structure has no rule (EXPERIENCE § Offline & Sync "Conflict rule"; § Block Model rules; Flow 3 failure; Flow 6). *Why it bites:* two offline devices can add/remove/move/reorder blocks, edit setup, captions, points of attention, or delete a sheet the other is editing, and "Manter a minha / dele" does not cover any of it. *Fix or question:* define per entity: sheet body = person-resolved; structure = mergeable (add-only set + fractional order index, last-writer-wins on setup), delete-vs-edit = keep both and surface; state which fields are inside the sheet document (photo links? location? not-tested?).
- **[critical]** PDF provenance is undefined: the brief sells an editable DOCX, but Flow 5 generates the PDF from app data after Bruno "changes nothing structural" in Word (EXPERIENCE Flow 5; § Export dialog; brief § Solution 5). *Why it bites:* any Word edit is silently absent from the client-facing PDF, or the architecture needs DOCX re-upload and a DOCX→PDF converter. *Fix or question:* decide: PDF = render of app data (edits must be made in the app) or PDF = conversion of the uploaded, edited DOCX; the answer changes the export pipeline and the revision model.
- **[critical]** Section 9 regrouping is not derivable (EXPERIENCE § Capture-to-Document, table row 9 and the paragraph after it). *Why it bites:* the Template has no locations, yet the rule "is a property of the Template"; the real document uses two schemes (small cabines: one subsection, all types in sheet order; large cabines: one subsection per type group across columns, TP+TC and Cabos+Trafo paired) plus a subsection-title pattern, none of which is stated. *Fix or question:* specify per cabine a flag "agrupar por tipo" with fixed group order (Seccionadoras · Disjuntores · TP's e TC's · Transformadores e Cabos), ordering = tree order, and the title pattern; or accept one subsection per cabine and confirm with Bruno that this is "no restructuring".
- **[critical]** Offline durability in a browser is under-specified for the "never lose a photo" bar (EXPERIENCE § Responsive & Platform "only device APIs required"; § Offline & Sync; State Patterns "Cold open, session, offline"). *Why it bites:* cold-open offline needs a service worker/PWA shell (not listed); iPadOS Safari evicts script-writable storage after 7 days without use unless installed to Home Screen and does not honor `persist()`; "background sync" only runs while the tab is foregrounded on iOS. *Fix or question:* add an "Instalar na tela inicial" step/surface, accept "sync runs while the app is open", and state a maximum offline age before photos must be uploaded.

### High

- **[high]** Template instantiation produces unplaced blocks with no state or surface (EXPERIENCE § Block Model "quantities"; § Block palette "ask for TAG and location before creating"; Flow 1 steps 3–5). *Why it bites:* "25 seccionadoras" from a template have neither TAG nor location; the model says equipment hangs off a location and TAG is mandatory at creation. *Fix or question:* add an "Sem local" staging bucket in the tree, or make templates carry a location skeleton; state that TAG is optional until placement.
- **[high]** Sub-block ownership and print effect are ambiguous (EXPERIENCE § Block Model sub-block row; "Print spec for section 9"; Flow 1 step 3; "Subtype defaults ... pre-mark NA"). *Why it bites:* three mechanisms overlap (template toggle, in-sheet toggle, subtype NA pre-mark) and the print spec says checklist lists are fixed verbatim; toggling "Motor" off either changes the corporate layout or is just an NA. *Fix or question:* rule: Template holds defaults, Laudo snapshots them at creation, per-sheet toggles override; a toggled-off checklist item prints as NA; a toggled-off test sub-block prints as "—" table or is omitted (pick one); say where subtype is set.
- **[high]** TAG identity scope is undefined (EXPERIENCE § Registries "Equipment identity"; § Sheet header "Tap TAG to edit"; § Block palette). *Why it bites:* copy-on-create by TAG needs a site-scoped Equipment entity; uniqueness per site collides on "COLUNA 2" (PTMT vs IM) and on two offline devices adding "SEC-09"; editing a TAG is a rename or a re-link. *Fix or question:* TAG unique per (site, cabine), enforced softly with a warning; Equipment is an entity under Site; TAG edit = rename of that entity; duplicates across devices resolved by the server flagging, not merging.
- **[high]** SE characteristics and environment: owner and print position contradict each other and the real document (EXPERIENCE § Laudo setup "visit environment"; § Block Model Location row "attributes of the cabine/visit"; § Capture-to-Document row 9 "first sheet of each cabine"; Flow 2 step 2; extraction § 2 Modelo 1). *Why it bites:* only 4 of 6 cabines in the real laudo carry the SE + environment block (1° Subsolo and Geradores do not); values are entered from inside a sheet but belong to the cabine, crossing the conflict unit. *Fix or question:* SE type/voltages/power and environment are Cabine attributes edited in the tree (cabine row), printed only on Modelo 1 (Cabos de entrada) sheets; confirm whether environment is per cabine or per visit.
- **[high]** Export revision semantics are incomplete (EXPERIENCE § Export dialog "Each export creates a numbered revision"; § Capture-to-Document row 7; State Patterns "Laudo exported, then edited"; Flow 5). *Why it bites:* DOCX then PDF of identical content would be revisions 1 and 2; the app does not say what photo numbers it shows after a post-export edit; no surface lists or downloads past revisions; the revision number is not printed anywhere in the document. *Fix or question:* revision = content snapshot, formats are renderings of a revision, new revision only when content changed; numbers become provisional again on any change; add a "Revisões" list to Export; decide whether the export revision prints on the cover.
- **[high]** Acceptance criteria hang off the Instrument (EXPERIENCE § Registries Instruments "default acceptance value"; § Block Model "Instruments store a default per test type"). *Why it bites:* contradicts the brief's "criteria as data with their source" structure and the SaaS story; the registry table has one default per instrument while the model text says per test type; a criterion is a property of test type/equipment/norm, not of the meter. *Fix or question:* Criterion entity (operator, value, unit, source) referenced by the test sub-block definition in the Template; the instrument only supplies voltage/current defaults; per-sheet override keeps its source.
- **[high]** The server cannot know another device's unsynced state; offline availability of a laudo on a device is unspecified (EXPERIENCE § Offline & Sync "Aguardando sincronização de Eduardo"; Flow 4 step 1 "cached last night on Wi-Fi"). *Why it bites:* "waiting for Eduardo's sync" is only knowable from Eduardo's last heartbeat; and nothing says which laudos, and whose photos (thumbnails vs originals), a device holds offline. *Fix or question:* downgrade to "Eduardo: última sincronização 14:20, N itens pendentes então"; add an explicit "Disponível offline" rule (e.g. laudos opened while online, photos of other devices as thumbnails only).

### Medium

- **[medium]** Tree "Order = capture order" is ambiguous (EXPERIENCE § Laudo tree). *Why it bites:* read as "sorted by fill time" the order changes under the user; read as "planned route" it is a stored per-laudo order, and the Template's "ordered composition" then has no location to order within. *Fix or question:* say "order is manual, per laudo, initially template order"; use a merge-friendly order key.
- **[medium]** Location depth is fixed at Cabine › Coluna but the data needs 1 or 3 levels (EXPERIENCE § Block Model Location; Flow 2 "Cubículo Enel › Cabos de entrada"; extraction § 2 9.2 "PTMT" vs "IM"). *Why it bites:* Enel has equipment directly under the cabine; 1° Subsolo has two panels (PTMT, IM) each with a "COLUNA 2". *Fix or question:* make Coluna optional and let the column name carry the panel, or allow a nested location.
- **[medium]** Status pill transitions are events without a full table (EXPERIENCE § Status pill; State Patterns "Laudo exported, then edited"). *Why it bites:* PDF-first export (Em campo → Emitido?), DOCX re-export after Emitido, a manual move back to Rascunho followed by a sheet touch, are all undefined. *Fix or question:* provide (state, event) → state, marking automatic transitions and whether they override manual backwards moves.
- **[medium]** Sheet state vs Progress counter is inconsistent (EXPERIENCE § Conclusion control "Required for Concluir ficha"; State Patterns "Not tested ... counts as complete"; Flow 5 step 1 lists a Não ensaiado sheet as "não concluída"). *Why it bites:* the generator and the pre-issue list need one rule for "concluded". *Fix or question:* Concluída requires Progress = Completa; Não ensaiada is complete and not listed as "não concluída".
- **[medium]** "Preenchido por" is overwritten on every autosave (EXPERIENCE § Sheet header "updated on every save with the current user"; § Interaction Primitives autosave 500 ms). *Why it bites:* a one-tap fix by Eduardo re-attributes Bruno's sheet on the printed document. *Fix or question:* store first-filled-by, last-modified-by and concluded-by; print concluded-by (fallback last-modified).
- **[medium]** Inline registry creation offline has no id/dedupe rule; Atividade and Local registries are missing (EXPERIENCE § Registries; § Combobox; § Caption composer "gender/number agreement from registry metadata"). *Why it bites:* two devices creating "Schneider"/"SCHNEIDER" need server-side merge and client-side reference rewriting; the caption grammar needs gender/number fields on registries that are not listed. *Fix or question:* store Manufacturer, Voltage class, Atividade and Local by value on the sheet (registry = suggestion list, client UUID, server dedupe by normalized name); reference by id only Instruments and Clients; add Atividade/Local registries with gender/number.
- **[medium]** Point-of-attention photo references under "Editar texto" (EXPERIENCE § Capture-to-Document row 8; § Point of attention card). *Why it bites:* a literal "Imagem 5" typed in free text goes stale when numbering changes between revisions. *Fix or question:* references are tokens resolved at generation; free text cannot embed numbers.
- **[medium]** "Removed block data recoverable until export" has no UI and no conflict rule (EXPERIENCE § Block Model rules; § Interaction Primitives Undo). *Why it bites:* after the 10 s toast nothing can recover it; purge "at export" is either rev-1 or every export; delete on device A vs edit on device B is unspecified. *Fix or question:* soft-delete with tombstone, purge policy = architecture; delete-vs-edit surfaces as a conflict.
- **[medium]** Photo chronological order across devices (EXPERIENCE § Capture-to-Document row 7; Flow 4 step 4). *Why it bites:* gallery picks carry EXIF time, not attach time; two devices need a deterministic tiebreak so re-exports never renumber. *Fix or question:* sort key = (capture time: EXIF if present else device time, device id, local sequence), in America/Sao_Paulo; document it.
- **[medium]** Export is shown as a synchronous download (EXPERIENCE § Export dialog "Result: file download"). *Why it bites:* 120 pages + 80 photos + PDF layout will take tens of seconds to minutes; the dialog needs a progress/async pattern and a place for the finished file. *Fix or question:* add "Gerando… pode fechar" + notification/toast, and the Revisões list.
- **[medium]** Project vs Client vs Site overlap (EXPERIENCE § IA Project "One client/site"; § Registries Clients "site(s)"). *Why it bites:* three entities for two concepts; TAG scope and copy-on-create depend on which one is stable. *Fix or question:* Project ≡ Site of a Client, or drop Project.
- **[medium]** Two users on one device and user provisioning (EXPERIENCE § Login form; § Foundation Identity). *Why it bites:* local queue and cached laudos must be namespaced per user; no surface creates users or resets passwords. *Fix or question:* state one-user-per-device-session with per-user local store; users seeded by the developer for the MVP.
- **[medium]** "Salvar como template" from a laudo is undefined (EXPERIENCE § IA Template composer). *Why it bites:* whether locations, TAGs and sub-block overrides are carried decides whether templates can hold a location skeleton (which would also fix the unplaced-blocks finding). *Fix or question:* define what is copied.

### Low

- **[low]** Not-tested sheets: print nameplate and checklist or only the reason band? Position among manual points in section 8? (EXPERIENCE § Capture-to-Document rows 8–9). *Fix:* print nameplate + reason band, no tables; list after manual points.
- **[low]** Section 11 sources: "instruments checked as used" in Laudo setup vs "every instrument referenced by any sheet" (EXPERIENCE § IA Laudo setup; § Capture-to-Document row 11). *Fix:* union, with the setup list only pre-selecting the picker.
- **[low]** Storage-low detection and "photo kept in memory for one retry" (State Patterns "Storage low"). *Why:* Safari quota estimates are coarse; an in-memory photo dies with the tab. *Fix:* keep the UX, let architecture pick the signal; on refusal try an immediate upload if online.
- **[low]** Photo link target: sheet vs checklist row (§ Checklist row "photo affordance"; § Point of attention "pre-links the photo"). *Fix:* photo has laudo + optional sheet + optional row.
- **[low]** "Duplicar" a block with data and a TAG (§ Block card). *Fix:* duplicate structure only, TAG blank.
- **[low]** Inter must be bundled for offline use (DESIGN § Typography). *Fix:* self-host or accept the fallback stack offline.

## Could be architecture's call

- Server-side generation and "export needs connection" (marked ASSUMPTION; UX only needs the offline message).
- Autosave debounce 500 ms; registry pagination at 100; storage-low threshold "< 2 photos' worth".
- "Photos stored as captured, full size" — print size is 8×6 cm; client-side downscale/compression is an architecture trade-off against upload time and quota, as long as the original survives until acknowledged.
- "Photos upload one at a time"; resumable/chunked upload; "sheet data first" is a fair UX priority, the mechanism is not.
- Conflict detection mechanism (version vectors vs base revision) and where unresolved versions live (client vs server).
- Snapshot vs versioned-reference for Template → Laudo block definitions ("edited without touching laudos" only fixes the observable behavior).
- Generated ToC and "Página X de Y": Word field update on open vs computed server-side; PDF rendering engine.
- Session token lifetime and offline re-auth mechanics.
- Purge/retention policy for removed-block data and past revisions.
- Sync trigger ("when a connection appears") and service-worker/PWA install as the vehicle for offline shell + storage persistence.
- Registry replication to the device for offline Comboboxes.

## Missing inputs for architecture

- Maximum photo resolution/size to keep; whether originals must be retained after the document is generated; accepted formats for certificate files and cover photo.
- Expected and maximum laudo size (brief: 94 sheets, ~80 photos, 120 pages) and number of active laudos per device.
- Concurrency expectations: max users/devices per laudo (2?), office editing concurrent with field, maximum offline age before sync.
- Export time tolerance and whether generation may be asynchronous with a notification.
- Whether tree order is per laudo (implied) and what the Template's "ordered composition" orders.
- Offline availability rule: which laudos and whose photos (thumbnails vs originals) a device holds.
- PDF provenance decision (render app data vs convert the edited DOCX).
- Section 9 grouping scheme and subsection title pattern; whether "one subsection per cabine" counts as restructuring for Bruno.
- Environment (temperature/humidity/altitude) per cabine or per visit.
- Whether the export revision number appears in the document, and where.
- Retention: how long generated revisions and removed data must stay downloadable/recoverable.
- Time zone/locale for timestamps and photo ordering (assume America/Sao_Paulo).
- User provisioning for the MVP (seeded users, password reset).
