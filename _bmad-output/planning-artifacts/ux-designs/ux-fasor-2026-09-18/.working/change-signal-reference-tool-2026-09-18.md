## Standing decisions that constrain the delta (do not reopen)

- Brand: the Fasor Engenharia brand appears only inside the generated document, never in the app; the app shows the literal placeholder PRODUTO (DESIGN.md § Brand & Style).
- Rejected — auto-verdicts: the app calculates, compares and highlights; the engineer concludes (EXPERIENCE.md § Inspiration & Anti-patterns). Generated text is therefore always a **suggestion the engineer confirms** (Smart Input › Suggestion field), never a verdict.
- PDF and DOCX are two renderings of app data, produced together as one export revision; text edits go back into the app. No local project file, no "save HTML": it is a SaaS (memlog).
- AI/OCR runs in the backend only; offline, work is queued.
- Single tenant in the MVP (Fasor); tenant onboarding has no surface. A company profile for the single tenant is allowed.
