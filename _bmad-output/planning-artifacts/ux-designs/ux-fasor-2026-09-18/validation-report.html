Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Scope: DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md (v0.1.0), reviewed against WCAG 2.2 AA plus field ergonomics (gloves, one hand, sunlight/dim cubicles, no signal, pt-BR). Contrast ratios recomputed from the frontmatter hex values; thresholds 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for UI boundaries and state indicators. Verdict, in the reviewer's words: the palette is unusually solid — every ratio the prose claims is numerically correct, every semantic ink passes 4.5:1 on white, on its own fill, and in the dark theme, and the "word next to every color" rule is honoured by almost every component. The gaps are behavioral rather than chromatic: the photo pending-upload dot is color-only and fails 3:1, the tri-state "retap to clear" is incompatible with the radio semantics the spine promises (and is a glove hazard), reorder has no single-pointer alternative (WCAG 2.5.7), several tap targets have no size, and the decimal-dot conversion rule can silently turn "3.700" into 3,7 MΩ. None of these needs a palette change; all need a sentence or two in the spine before the components are built. Three computed failures drove the critical/high items: photo pending dot 2.62:1 light / 1.62:1 dark; dark-theme toast action 1.93:1; disabled primary button 1.36:1 (exempt, but invisible in sun). What is already strong: whole-field focus (8.66:1 border), 56px field controls, no swipe gestures, no hover-only affordances, and a non-blocking design that never stops navigation on expiry, out-of-limit or offline.

Architecture-lens review

review-architecture.md

Reviewed: DESIGN.md, EXPERIENCE.md, imports/extract-fo-serv-03.md, the brief. Verdict, in the reviewer's words: the spine is implementable as a product and its Block Model, sheet behavior and registries are 80% specifiable; the seed template plus the extraction give the generator almost everything for sections 0–8, 10, 11. Three things will bite the architecture if built as written: the conflict model covers only sheet bodies while the laudo's structure (tree, blocks, order, photo links, points of attention, setup) can diverge across offline devices; section 9 regrouping and the SE/environment placement cannot be derived from what is written; and the export/revision story (Word-edited DOCX vs. PDF from app data, revision per format, numbering after post-export edits) is unresolved and sits on the critical path of "ready to sign". A fourth, offline-in-a-browser, is under-specified relative to the "never lose a photo" bar. The reviewer also listed what could be architecture's call (server-side generation, autosave debounce, photo compression, conflict detection mechanism, ToC engine, session lifetime, retention, sync trigger/PWA vehicle, registry replication) — v0.2.0 collects these under a new "Handed to architecture" section — and the missing inputs for architecture (photo size and retention, laudo size, concurrency, export time tolerance, timezone, provisioning), now phrased as questions in EXPERIENCE.md § Open Questions.
