## Corrections to Round 1 and to the brief's assumptions (read first)

- **C1. The Table 100.1 numbering is right for breakers, switches and cables, but not for transformers or instrument transformers.** In MTS-2019 those use **Table 100.5** ("Transformer Insulation Resistance Maintenance Testing"). See F6.
- **C2. The "winding resistance within one percent" rule is not what MTS says.** MTS-2019 §7.2.2, liquid-filled, says: "Temperature corrected winding-resistance values should compare within **two percent of previously obtained results**." The 1% figure is believed to come from the acceptance standard (ATS). This is not verified this run.
- **C3. The vacuum bottle integrity test does not use Table 100.19 in MTS-2019.** §7.6.3.B.7 says the test is performed "in strict accordance with manufacturer's published data". Table 100.19 only applies to the *optional* dielectric withstand test with the breaker closed (§7.6.3.B.9).
- **C4. The IR temperature correction is Table 100.14, split into two parts.** 100.14.1 corrects to 20 °C and 100.14.2 corrects to 40 °C. Each part has separate columns for oil-immersed and solid insulation. The table credits its source as "Derived from Megger, *A Stitch in Time*".

## Findings

### Q3: NETA MTS-2019, medium-voltage sections

**F1. §7.6.3 Circuit Breakers, Vacuum, Medium-Voltage: electrical tests**
- **Tests listed:**
  - Resistance of bolted connections with a low-resistance ohmmeter.
  - IR for 1 minute on each pole: phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground with the breaker closed, and across each open pole. Voltage per manufacturer, or Table 100.1 if no data.
  - *Optional:* control-wiring IR at 500 V DC (300 V cable) or 1000 V DC (600 V cable), for 1 minute.
  - Static contact/pole-resistance test.
  - In the test position: trip and close with the control switch, **trip the breaker by operating each of its protective relays**, and verify the charge, trip-free and antipump functions.
  - *Optional:* minimum pickup voltage of the trip and close coils (Table 100.20).
  - Vacuum bottle integrity test with the breaker open, "in strict accordance with manufacturer's published data".
  - *Optional:* MAC (magnetron atmospheric condition) test.
  - *Optional:* dielectric withstand with the breaker closed (manufacturer data, or Table 100.19).
  - Heaters.
- **Visual/mechanical checks (V&M) also listed:**
  - Contact timing (required).
  - *Optional:* first-trip test, mechanism-motion analysis and coil current signature.
  - Record the operation counter as-found and as-left.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F2. §7.6.3 "Test Values – Electrical" (vacuum MV breaker)**
- Bolted connections: investigate values that deviate from similar connections by more than 50% of the lowest value.
- IR per manufacturer, or Table 100.1.
- **Control wiring IR: "comparable to previously obtained results but not less than two megohms."**
- **Contact resistance:** "Microhm or dc millivolt drop values shall not exceed the high levels of the normal range as indicated in the manufacturer's published data. If manufacturer's data is not available, investigate values that deviate from adjacent poles or similar breakers by more than 50 percent of the lowest value."
- Vacuum bottle integrity and dielectric withstand are pass/fail: pass if "no evidence of distress or insulation failure" by the end of voltage application.
- MAC: evaluate per the test-equipment maker's instructions.
- Operation counter "should advance one digit per close-open cycle".
- Contact timing, travel and coil current: per manufacturer data and previous test data.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F3. §7.5.1.2 Switches, Air, Medium-Voltage, Metal-Enclosed**
- **Tests:** contact resistance across each switchblade assembly **and fuseholder**; IR per pole for 1 minute (Table 100.1); dielectric withstand with the switch closed (manufacturer data or **Table 100.2**); **fuse resistance**; heaters; *optional* online partial-discharge survey.
- **Test values:**
  - Contact resistance: same "high end of the manufacturer's normal range, else 50% of the lowest" rule, compared across adjacent poles or similar switches.
  - "Dielectric withstand voltage tests shall not proceed until insulation-resistance levels are raised above minimum values."
  - **"Investigate fuse resistance values that deviate from each other by more than 15 percent."**
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F4. §7.2.2 Transformers, Liquid-Filled: electrical tests and values**
- **Tests:**
  - IR winding-to-winding and each winding-to-ground, with the polarization index (PI) calculated. Voltage per manufacturer, or Table 100.5.
  - Turns ratio at the designated tap.
  - Power factor (PF) or dissipation factor (DF) of the windings; bushing PF/capacitance, or hot-collar if there is no tap.
  - Excitation current.
  - *Optional:* sweep frequency response analysis (SFRA).
  - Winding resistance at the designated tap.
  - *Optional:* core insulation at 500 V DC.
  - Oil sample per ASTM D923 for D1816 (breakdown), D974 (acidity), D971 (interfacial tension, IFT), D1500 (colour), D1524 (visual), D1533 (water) and, *optionally*, D924 (PF).
  - Dissolved gas analysis (DGA) per IEEE C57.104 or ASTM D3612.
- **Values:**
  - IR ≥ Table 100.5, or the manufacturer's value.
  - **PI compared with previous results and "should not be less than 1.0".**
  - **Turns ratio "should not deviate by more than one-half percent from either the adjacent coils or the calculated ratio".**
  - Winding resistance within **2%** of previous results, temperature-corrected.
  - Bushing PF: investigate if more than 50% from nameplate. Bushing capacitance: investigate if more than 5% from nameplate. Hot-collar: investigate above 0.1 W.
  - Excitation current, three-leg core: the expected pattern is "two similar current readings and one lower current reading".
  - Core insulation ≥ 1 MΩ at 500 V DC.
  - Oil per Table 100.4.
  - DGA evaluated per IEEE C57.104.
  - The V&M section also covers checking the alarm and trip circuits of the temperature and level indicators, the pressure-relief device and the gas/fault-pressure relay.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F6. Table 100.5, transformer IR minimums (MTS-2019)**

| Winding rating | Minimum DC test voltage | Min. IR, liquid-filled | Min. IR, dry |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–600 V | 1,000 V | 100 MΩ | 500 MΩ |
| 601–5,000 V | 2,500 V | 1,000 MΩ | 5,000 MΩ |
| >5,000 V | **5,000 V** | **5,000 MΩ** | **25,000 MΩ** |

- Note: "Since insulation resistance depends on insulation rating (kV) and winding capacity (kVA), values obtained should be compared to manufacturer's published data."
- Temperature correction uses Table 100.14.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F7. Table 100.1 (MTS-2019) confirms Round 1's values and adds a note on cables**
- Values: 15,000 V equipment → 2,500 V DC / 5,000 MΩ; 25,000 V → 5,000 V / 10,000 MΩ; 34,000 V → 5,000 V / 100,000 MΩ.
- Note 6: for MV cables "the values shown are typical. Conductor and insulation geometry, temperature, and overall cable length, need to be taken into account per manufacturers published data for definitive minimum insulation resistance criteria."
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F8. Table 100.14, IR temperature correction**
- **100.14.1 (to 20 °C):** Rc = Ra × K.
  - Oil-immersed: 20 °C → 1.00; 25 → 1.40; 30 → 1.98; 35 → 2.80; 40 → 3.95; 45 → 5.60; 50 → 7.85; 60 → 15.85.
  - Solid insulation: 25 → 1.25; 30 → 1.58; 35 → 2.00; 40 → 2.50; 50 → 3.98.
  - Below 20 °C (oil): 15 → 0.75; 10 → 0.50; 5 → 0.36; 0 → 0.25.
- **100.14.2** gives the same data normalised to 40 °C.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F12. §7.3.3 Shielded Cables, Medium- and High-Voltage**
- **Tests:** IR on each conductor with the other conductors and shields grounded (Table 100.1 or manufacturer data); shield-continuity test by ohmmeter.
- **Withstand/diagnostic tests:** DC, VLF or 50/60 Hz withstand; tan δ; PD. MTS says these do not denote optional or required, and the method is chosen with the cable owner.
- **Values:**
  - IR ≥ Table 100.1 or the manufacturer's value.
  - **"Shielding shall exhibit continuity. Investigate resistance values in excess of ten ohms per 1000 feet of cable."**
  - Withstand is pass/fail (no distress).
  - Diagnostics: "refer to applicable standards or manufacturer's literature".
- Minimum bend radius per Table 100.22.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F13. §7.10.1 Current Transformers (MTS-2019)**
- **Tests:** IR of each CT and its wiring to ground at **1000 V DC, 1 minute**. *Optional:* polarity, ratio verification (voltage or current method) and excitation test for relaying CTs, all per IEEE C57.13.1. *Optional:* burden measurement. Primary IR with the secondary grounded per Table 100.5. Dielectric withstand per Table 100.9. *Optional:* PF/DF. Verify there is **only one grounding point** (IEEE C57.13.3).
- **Values:**
  - IR ≥ Table 100.5.
  - "Polarity results should agree with transformer markings."
  - "Ratio errors should not be greater than values shown in IEEE C57.13."
  - Excitation should match the manufacturer's curve, or IEEE C57.13.1.
  - Burden compared with the CT rating.
  - One grounding point only.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F14. §7.10.2 Voltage Transformers (MTS-2019)**
- **Tests:** IR for 1 minute winding-to-winding and winding-to-ground (Table 100.5). *Optional:* polarity (H1–X1), turns ratio at the as-found tap, burden, dielectric withstand for 1 minute (Table 100.9), PF/DF. Single grounding point.
- **Values** ("in accordance with IEEE C57.13 8.1.1"):
  - Revenue metering: ratio error **≤ ±0.1%**, phase angle **±0.9 mrad (3 minutes)**.
  - **Other applications: ≤ 1.2% ratio, ±17.5 mrad (1°) phase angle.**
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion.

**F15. §7.9.1 / §7.9.2 Protective relays (electromechanical and solid-state / microprocessor)**
- **Overcurrent elements:**
  - **50 (instantaneous overcurrent):** determine pickup and dropout; *optional:* time delay.
  - **51 (time overcurrent):** "Determine minimum pickup. Determine time delay at **two points on the time current curve**."
  - Also 50BF (breaker failure): current-supervision pickup, time delays, and all initiate inputs and outputs used.
- **Other checks:** targets and LEDs; IR circuit-to-frame (for solid-state relays, per manufacturer).
- **Microprocessor relays:** download events, SOE and settings before testing; verify the relay's date and time; check that analog readings are within the manufacturer's tolerances.
- **Values:**
  - Elements are "calibrated using **manufacturer's recommended tolerances** unless critical test points are specified by the setting engineer".
  - "As-left relay settings should match the most recent coordination and arc-flash study or engineered setting files."
  - Relay elements should operate contacts and operate or block end devices "as per the design".
- **Trip via the breaker** is required under §7.6.3.B.5.2 ("Trip breaker by operating each of its protective relays").
- NETA gives **no generic % tolerance**. Tolerances come from the relay manufacturer.
- Confidence: high. Class: technical-criterion and workflow.

### Q4: Contact-resistance test current

**F16. IEC 62271-1:2007 §6.4.1 (type test) and §7.3 (routine test), read in the Indian adoption IS/IEC 62271-1:2007 (identical text)**
- The main-circuit resistance is measured "with d.c. by measuring the voltage drop or resistance across the terminals of each pole … **The current during the measurement shall have any convenient value between 50 A and the rated normal current.**"
- NOTE: "an increase of the main circuit resistance cannot alone be considered as reliable evidence of bad contacts … the measurement should be repeated with a higher current, as close as possible to the rated normal current."
- Type test: after the temperature-rise test, resistance must not increase by more than 20%.
- **Routine test: "The measured resistance shall not exceed 1,2 × R_u", where R_u is the value measured before the type-test temperature rise.**
- Implication for the app: record the test current (A) and the result in µΩ, and use the manufacturer's value (R_u-based) × 1.2 as a limit where one is known.
- Source: https://archive.org/stream/gov.in.is.iec.62271.1.2007/is.iec.62271.1.2007_djvu.txt (BIS / IEC, 2007).
- Note: the IEC 62271-1:2017 edition was not checked. The secondary site insulationtesting.com says the "≤50 A → rated current" and "1.2 × Ru" rules are unchanged.
- Confidence: high for 2007, medium that 2017 is the same. Class: technical-criterion.

### Q1: NBR 10576:2017

**F17. What was found: Brazilian procurement practice (CESAN, the Espírito Santo state water utility)**
- The CESAN technical service prescriptions (2019) specify: "ENSAIOS DO GRUPO 1 (cor e aparência, fator de perdas dielétricas, índice de neutralização, rigidez dielétrica, tensão interfacial e teor de água). NORMA: ABNT NBR 10576:2017", with sampling per NBR 8840.
- The same document specifies:
  - DGA sampling per NBR 7070:2006.
  - DBDS per NBR 16412:2015.
  - IR with instruments at "1KV, 2,5KV, 5KV E 10KV".
    - Transformer IR connections: AT×BT, AT×massa and BT×massa, using the guard terminal.
    - Breaker IR above 1 kV: R×R1, S×S1, T×T1 with the breaker open, and R×M, S×M, T×M, R×S, S×T, T×R with it closed.
  - Relay testing: remove the relay, clean it, test functions "50/51/51G/27/46", then "simular desligamento do disjuntor em posição de teste".
  - Buchholz test with slow and fast airflow.
  - Thermography of substations "até 34,5 kV".
  - Every service ends with: "emitir laudo e gráfico histórico dos valores medidos. Determinar necessidade de ações de manutenção, informando o risco".
- Implication: the 2017 edition's six **Group 1** tests are the reference set for the laudo's oil sheet.
- Source: https://www.cesan.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/ANEXO-VIII.pdf.pdf (CESAN, 2019-06-25).
- Confidence: high that the practice exists. Class: workflow and market-practice.

### Q6: Relay test

**F19. Brazilian test-set practice (CONPROVE tutorial for the Pextron URP 6000, directional overcurrent)**
- The test set records pickup, dropout, the percentage and absolute errors to pass or fail each point, and operating times on a time sequence.
- The current, time and angle tolerances "devem ser consultadas no manual do fabricante do relé".
- This agrees with NETA F15: there is no universal ±% figure.
- Source: https://conprove.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Tutorial_Teste_Rele_PEXTRON_URP6000_Direcional_de_Sobrecorrente_CTC.pdf (CONPROVE, 2022).
- Confidence: high. Class: workflow and market-practice.

### Q3 (market practice): Vacuum-integrity test voltage in the field

**F20. TestGuy forum**
- Practitioners report that NETA 2019 lists 27 kV for this test. They report GE breakers specifying "36 kV for 10 seconds", and a 27 kV-class VCP-W breaker requiring more than 40 kV.
- They agree the manufacturer's value governs, and that acceptance and maintenance use the same value.
- **This conflicts with F1/C3**, where MTS-2019 itself defers to the manufacturer. The "27 kV" figure matches Table 100.2 for 15 kV metal-clad switchgear, so practitioners probably apply Table 100.2 or 100.19 by default. **Treat it as forum hearsay.**
- Source: https://forum.testguy.net/threads/6655-15kv-vacuum-breaker-maintenance-hipot-test-voltage (TestGuy forum, undated).
- Confidence: low. Class: market-practice.

## Implications for the data model (derived from F1–F19, not new evidence)

- **Breaker sheet:**
  - IR per pole, closed and open: kV applied, MΩ, temperature, corrected MΩ.
  - Control-wiring IR: pass if ≥ 2 MΩ.
  - Contact resistance per pole: µΩ and test current in A (≥ 50 A).
  - Criterion: the manufacturer's limit, else a deviation check at 50% of the lowest pole.
  - Vacuum integrity per bottle: kV, duration, pass/fail, and a field citing the manufacturer's document.
  - Trip via relay, trip-free and antipump: pass/fail.
  - Operation counter as-found and as-left.
  - Optional timing (ms).
- **Switch sheet:** contact resistance per pole and fuseholder; fuse resistance with a ≤15% spread check; IR.
- **Transformer sheet:**
  - IR in MΩ against Table 100.5 (5 kV DC for windings above 5 kV), with the 20 °C correction and PI (≥1.0).
  - TTR: ≤0.5% versus calculated ratio or adjacent phases.
  - Winding resistance: ≤2% versus previous (needs history).
  - Excitation-current pattern.
  - Link to the oil report: the six NBR 10576:2017 Group 1 tests, plus DGA.
- **Cable sheet:** IR per phase; shield continuity (≤ 10 Ω/1000 ft ≈ 32.8 Ω/km, converted by the author).
- **CT/VT sheet:** IR at 1 kV DC; polarity against markings; ratio error; excitation curve; single grounding point.
- **Relay sheet:** as-found and as-left settings; 50 pickup and dropout; 51 pickup plus two curve points (measured time vs. theoretical); the manufacturer's tolerance as an input field; breaker trip confirmed.
