## Findings

1. **NETA Table 100.1, insulation resistance by equipment rating (apparatus other than rotating machinery, values at 20 °C):**

   | Nominal rating | Minimum DC test voltage | Recommended minimum IR |
   |---|---|---|
   | 250 V | 500 V | 25 MΩ |
   | 600 V | 1,000 V | 100 MΩ |
   | 1,000 V | 1,000 V | 100 MΩ |
   | 2,500 V | 1,000 V | 500 MΩ |
   | 5,000 V | 2,500 V | 1,500 MΩ |
   | 8,000 V | 2,500 V | 2,500 MΩ |
   | **15,000 V** | **2,500 V** | **5,000 MΩ** |
   | **25,000 V** | **5,000 V** | **10,000 MΩ** |
   | **34,500 V** | **5,000 V** | **100,000 MΩ** |
   | 46,000 V and up | 5,000 V | 100,000 MΩ |

   NETA calls these "representative values suggested by the NETA Standards Review Council", to be used "in the absence of consensus standards". **Correction to the brief's hypothesis:** NETA's *minimum* test voltage for the 15 kV class is 2.5 kV DC, not 5 or 10 kV. 5 kV DC is NETA's minimum for the 25 and 34.5 kV classes.
   - Source: https://forum.testguy.net/content/240-Insulation-Resistance-Test-Values (TestGuy, which cites ANSI/NETA ATS-2017 and MTS-2015)
   - Cross-check: Quizlet sets labelled "ATS 2017 and MTS 2019" give the same values.
   - pub_date ~2017-2019; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: high that these values are Table 100.1; medium on the exact edition (the current NETA editions are ATS-2025 / MTS-2023, not checked)
   - class: technical-criterion

2. **Megger "A Stitch in Time" (Copyright 2006), Table I: insulation condition by ratio.**

   | Condition | DAR (60 s / 30 s) | PI (10 min / 1 min) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Dangerous | not given | < 1 |
   | Questionable | 1.0 to 1.25 | 1.0 to 2 |
   | Good | 1.4 to 1.6 | 2 to 4 |
   | Excellent | > 1.6 | > 4 |

   Megger itself says these values "must be considered tentative and relative". The table leaves DAR 1.25 to 1.4 unassigned. Suggested app encoding: flag "questionável" if DAR ≤ 1.25 or PI < 2; flag "perigoso" if PI < 1.
   - Source: https://www.instrumart.com/assets/Megger-Guide-to-Insulation-Testing.pdf (Megger)
   - pub_date 2006; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: high
   - class: technical-criterion

3. **Megger on test voltages and the "one-megohm rule".**
   - Routine maintenance test voltages: "4,160 volts and above: 1,000 to 5,000 volts, or higher".
   - The one-megohm rule: "Insulation resistance should be approximately one megohm for each 1,000 volts of operating voltage, with a minimum value of one megohm." Megger admits it is "somewhat arbitrary, and may be criticized as lacking an engineering foundation".
   - The formula **Rm = kV + 1 (MΩ, referred to 40 °C)** comes from the IEEE guide for **rotating machinery** (IEEE 43), quoted by Megger.
   - Implication: the Brazilian market's "1 MΩ/kV + 1" is borrowed from the motor standard (IEEE 43). It is not a criterion for cables, switchgear or transformers.
   - Megger gives bushings a threshold of **10,000 MΩ at 20 °C** (outdoor oil-breaker and transformer bushings).
   - Source: same Megger guide (sections "Test Voltage vs. Equipment Rating", "The One-Megohm Rule", "Bushings")
   - pub_date 2006; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: high (content); interpretation of the market origin: medium
   - class: technical-criterion

4. **Temperature correction (Megger Table II).**
   - Rule of thumb: "For every 10°C increase in temperature, halve the resistance; or, for every 10°C decrease, double the resistance."
   - Table II has factors per material. Examples for **oil-filled transformers, corrected to 20 °C:** 10 °C → 0.50; 25 °C → 1.40; 30 °C → 1.98; 35 °C → 2.80; 40 °C → 3.95; 50 °C → 7.85; 60 °C → 15.85.
   - For **cables**, Table II uses a 15.6 °C (60 °F) reference, with separate columns per insulation type (impregnated paper, GR-S, etc.). Megger: "the important point is to be consistent and correct to the same base."
   - The app should store the measured temperature, the insulation-type factor table and the corrected value.
   - Source: same Megger guide
   - confidence: high
   - class: technical-criterion

   | Test | Method | Limit |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rigidez dielétrica, eletrodo calota | ABNT NBR IEC 60156 | **≥ 40 kV** (50 / 60) |
   | Teor de água, corrigido para 20 °C | ABNT NBR 10710 | **≤ 25 ppm** (15 / 10) |
   | Fator de dissipação | ABNT NBR 12133 | ≤ 0.5 % at 25 °C; ≤ 15 % at 90 °C |
   | Fator de potência | ABNT NBR 12133 | ≤ 20 % at 100 °C |
   | Índice de neutralização | ABNT NBR 14248 | **≤ 0.15 mg KOH/g** |
   | Tensão interfacial at 25 °C | ABNT NBR 6234 | **≥ 22 mN/m** (22 / 25) |
   | Ponto de fulgor | ABNT NBR 11341 | maximum decrease of 10 °C |
   | Aparência | Visual | "Claro, isento de materiais em suspensão" |
   | Sedimentos | Anexo A | none detectable (<0.02 % by mass ignored) |
   | Inibidor DBPC | NBR 12134 | re-inhibit at 0.09 % |

   - Source: https://www.cgti.org.br/publicacoes/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/OS-NOVOS-PARA%CC%82METROS-DE-AVALIAC%CC%A7A%CC%83O-DA-QUALIDADE-DO-O%CC%81LEO-MINERAL-ISOLANTE-EM-SERVIC%CC%A7O-TRAZIDAS-PELA-RECENTE-REVISA%CC%83O-DA-NBR-10576-06-O%CC%81LEO-MINERAL-ISOLANTE-DE-EQUIPAMENTOS-ELE%CC%81TRICOS.pdf
   - Publisher: SNPTEE / GMI 04, Galdeano (MGM Consultoria) and Nunes Jr (Nynas)
   - pub_date 2007-10; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: **medium**. These are secondary reproductions of the **2006** edition. The current edition is NBR 10576:2017 (confirmed by the Target catalogue and Studocu listings). Its values were not verified this run and may differ.
   - class: technical-criterion (ABNT)

6. **NBR 10576:2006, other tables (same source, same caveats).**
   - **Table 1, reference values for new equipment before energization (≤72.5 kV):**
     - Rigidez ≥ 60 kV (calota, IEC 60156)
     - Água ≤ 15 ppm
     - TIF ≥ 40 mN/m
     - Índice de neutralização ≤ 0.03 mg KOH/g
     - Cor ≤ 1.0 (NBR 14483)
     - FD ≤ 0.05 % at 25 °C and ≤ 0.70 % at 90 °C
   - **Table 4, transformadores de instrumentos ≤145 kV:** rigidez ≥ 60 kV; água ≤ 15 ppm; FD ≤ 4.0 % at 90 °C.
   - **Table 5, disjuntores (PVO), all voltage classes:** rigidez ≥ **20 kV** (NBR 6869, disc electrode) or ≥ **30 kV** (NBR IEC 60156, calota).
   - **Table 6, comutadores:** rigidez ≥ 25 kV (neutral) or 40 kV (line); água ≤ 30 / 25 ppm.
   - **NBR 5416:** água ≤ 35 ppm at 60 °C sample temperature for class < 242 kV.
   - confidence: medium
   - class: technical-criterion

7. **Oil test standards used in Brazil (vocabulary).**
   - Rigidez dielétrica: ABNT NBR IEC 60156 (calota). The older method is NBR 6869 (disco).
   - Teor de água: NBR 10710 (Karl Fischer).
   - Tensão interfacial: NBR 6234.
   - Acidez / índice de neutralização: NBR 14248.
   - Cor: NBR 14483.
   - Fator de perdas / dissipação: NBR 12133.
   - Contagem de partículas: NBR 14275.
   - Corrective actions (market practice): termovácuo when água or rigidez are out of limit; regeneração or troca when TIF, acidez or FD are out of limit.
   - Sources: CGTI/SNPTEE (above); Trafocare (market-practice blog) https://trafocare.com.br/tratamento-termo-vacuo-para-purificacao-do-oleo-isolante/
   - accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: high (method numbers), medium (actions)
   - class: vocabulary / market-practice

8. **Contact resistance, NETA criterion.**
   - Verbatim: "Microhm or millivolt drop values shall not exceed the high levels of the normal range as indicated in the manufacturer's published data. If manufacturer's data is not available, investigate any values which deviate from adjacent poles or similar breakers by more than 50 percent of the lowest value."
   - Related NETA items in the same text: record "as-found and as-left operation counter readings"; IR "on each pole, phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground with the circuit breaker closed and across each open pole for one minute"; control wiring IR "not less than two megohms".
   - Source: http://www.hvserviceinc.com/userfiles/file/pdfs/neta-acceptance-7-6-1-2.pdf. This is NETA **MTS-2001**, the section for **low-voltage air power breakers**. In that edition the IR table is numbered 10.1, not 100.1.
   - The same 50 % criterion appears in the MV breaker sections of later NETA editions, per an Eng-Tips thread on MTS-2011 (search snippet only, not read in depth).
   - pub_date 2001; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: high (wording); medium (that the MV sections use identical wording)
   - class: technical-criterion

9. **Contact resistance, Brazilian academic view.** A UTFPR thesis (citing Ciarlini 2022) says the breaker standards "não estabelecem valores mínimos ou de referências de resistência de contato". It proposes three acceptable references:
   - (a) the fabricante's factory test (TAF);
   - (b) the history of earlier maintenance tests;
   - (c) "comparação direta ... entre polos e entre equipamentos".

   The procedure raises the current to about 90 % of the micro-ohmmeter's full scale until the reading stabilizes. NBR 7102 is cited for HV breaker tests.
   - Typical MV breaker values of **50 to 200 µΩ/pole at 100 A DC** appeared only in a search-engine summary. This is an **unverified belief**.
   - Source: https://repositorio.utfpr.edu.br/jspui/bitstream/1/30532/1/testesensaiossubestacaomedia.pdf (UTFPR, Assmann)
   - pub_date 2022; accessed 2026-09-18
   - confidence: medium
   - class: market-practice / technical-criterion

10. **Relação de transformação tolerance (transformer).** The UTFPR thesis reproduces an NBR 5356 table (ABNT 2007 edition): "Relação de tensões em qualquer derivação: ±0,5%" or "±1/10 da impedância de curto-circuito, expressa em porcentagem", whichever is smaller ("Deve ser aplicada a menor das tolerâncias indicadas").
    - The ratio must be checked on all taps with the transformer "operando em vazio".
    - A search summary also names **ABNT NBR 5356-1:2019** as the current reference for TTR and winding resistance.
    - Winding-resistance results are corrected to a reference temperature with the usual formula R_ref = R_meas × (T_ref + 234.5 or 225) / (T_meas + 234.5 or 225). The rendered equation was garbled, so the constants are not verified.
    - Source: UTFPR thesis (above)
    - confidence: medium (secondary reproduction of ABNT)
    - class: technical-criterion

11. **Bushing criteria used by concessionárias** (UTFPR thesis citing Rambo et al. 2007), relative to nameplate values:

    | Parameter | Aceitável | Medir com mais frequência | Retirar de operação |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Capacitância | 1 to 1.05 × C_placa | 1.05 to 1.1 × C_placa | > 1.1 × C_placa |
    | Fator de potência | 1 to 2 × FP_placa | 2 to 3 × FP_placa | > 3 × FP_placa |

    This is relevant only if the cabine's transformer has capacitive bushings, which is rare at 13.8 to 34.5 kV.
    - confidence: medium
    - class: technical-criterion

12. **Grounding measurement method.** ABNT NBR 15749 (2009) "Medição de resistência de aterramento e de potenciais na superfície do solo", items 5 and 6, sets out the **método da queda de potencial**: current is injected through an auxiliary current electrode, and the voltage to remote earth is measured with a potential probe moved step by step to build the curve. The 62 % rule (Tagg) and the curve-intersection method are the alternatives.
    - The app should capture: electrode distances, the curve points and the plateau value, not a single number.
    - Source: UTFPR thesis (secondary on NBR 15749)
    - confidence: medium
    - class: technical-criterion

13. **Grounding acceptance value (10 Ω) has no firm basis in the ABNT norms.**
    - Search-result snippets: NBR 14039 "não define um valor a ser atendido, porém recomenda um valor de 10 ohms" (canalsolar.com.br).
    - The old NBR 5419:2005 (SPDA) quoted 10 Ω for non-natural electrodes. NBR 5419:2015 **removed** it and no longer requires the measurement in periodic inspections (osetoreletrico.com.br; felixengenharia.com.br).
    - The expected criterion is **step and touch potentials within safe limits**, not a fixed Ω value.
    - One concessionária snippet (Enel via Bohnen, HV) requires a "relatório de comissionamento da malha" containing "o valor da resistência medida, o cálculo das tensões de passo, toque".
    - **Unverified:** whether any concessionária norm for 13.8 to 34.5 kV consumer cabines sets a hard Ω limit. The Roraima norm fetch failed.
    - Sources: https://canalsolar.com.br/projeto-eletrico-e-procedimento-de-conexao-de-usina-solar-em-media-tensao/ ; https://www.osetoreletrico.com.br/como-ficou-o-jargao-10-ohms-em-qualquer-epoca-do-ano-para-o-caso-de-aterramento-nao-natural-com-a-nova-abnt-nbr-5419/ ; https://bohnen.com.br/aterramento-em-subestacoes-de-alta-tensao-normas-da-enel-e-a-essencia-da-seguranca-eletrica/
    - accessed 2026-09-18
    - confidence: **low** (snippets only, not read in depth)
    - class: market-practice / regulatory

14. **NETA workflow conventions (Q7, brief).**
    - NETA procedures are structured as "1. Visual and Mechanical Inspection / 2. Electrical Tests / 3. Test Values". "Test Values" compares results with "manufacturer's published data", NETA tables, "previously obtained results" and "similar units".
    - Items include "Prior to cleaning the unit, perform as-found tests" and "Record as-found and as-left operation counter readings". Some tests are marked optional (*).
    - Source: NETA MTS-2001 text (hvserviceinc PDF)
    - confidence: high
    - class: workflow

It cites NBR 5356-1:2019 (resistência ôhmica, relação) and "NBR 7274:2017 (resistência de isolamento e índice de polarização)".
