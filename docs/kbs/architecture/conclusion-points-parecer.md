# Conclusion, points and parecer

The rules that hold on every seed. Version-specific rules: on v14 a failure floors Reprovado with no restriction and the parecer names the finding ([seed-v14](/architecture/seed-v14.md)); from v15 on, the refusals and the hand-chosen Reprovado ([seed-v15-v19](/architecture/seed-v15-v19.md)). The readings' verdicts come from [criteria-and-judgments](/architecture/criteria-and-judgments.md). The dated notes below were moved unchanged from `kernel-and-seed.md` on 2026-11-28; a note marked "planning, not built" is kept as history beside the "as built" note that followed it.

*(2026-11-06, Stories 22.4 to 22.6 as built, batch e22c (contract 31, minimum 31): the conclusion, the points and the parecer follow the fichas. `relatorio/conclusion.ts` `conclusionFloor` (an out reading, a ratio INSATISFATÓRIO, a measurement cell out or a `defect` Não ensaiado reason give Reprovado · Com restrições; an NC row or an observation Aprovado · Com restrições; else Aprovado · Sem restrições once judged) and `effectiveConclusionPair` (the stricter of floor and stored, per dimension) are what every reader shows and prints; `conclusionAdjusted` flags a stored pair a later floor made stricter; "Concluir ficha" writes the effective pair. A seed's not-tested reason gains the optional `defect` flag (`seed/defect-reason.ts` `isDefectReason`), set by no shipped seed. `points/derived.ts` derives `nc`, `reading` and `reprovado` entries beside `not_tested`, suppressed by a point of the same `origin` and source (`item_key`, `test_key`); `possibleDuplicatePoints` and `resolvedOriginPoints` are the Pontos flags. `relatorio/parecer.ts` `derivedParecer` and `effectiveParecer` replace the suggestion, the summary is always `composeParecer`'s and an earlier typed one is `parecerOwnText`; the "Parecer não preenchido" and site-NC-without-point pre-issue rows retired. `print/section-10.ts` drops the third fixed bullet under Não apto and reads "nos itens 8 e 9" by the printed positions.)*

*(2026-11-10, fix batch e24h (contract 41, minimum 41; Matheus, on Bruno's report of points created with no tap): a point of attention exists only when the technician creates it, on every seed. The kernel no longer lists, counts or prints the derived entries of Stories 6.6, 22.5, 23.6 and 23.11 (`derivedPoints`, `groupDerivedPoints`, `possibleDuplicate*`, `derivedPointSeed` and `derivedPointKindLabel` are gone): `points/summary.ts` `sectionEightEntries`, `pointsSummary` and `pointsHeadingText`, `print/section-8.ts`, the pre-issue rows (`points_possible_duplicate` retired) and the parecer's point counts read the stored live points only. The findings themselves stay internal in `points/derived.ts` (`allDerivedPoints`) for two readers that must not move: the parecer's floor (`hasPointSource`, so no verdict changes) and "Origem resolvida" (`resolvedOriginPoints`). The create paths are unchanged: "Criar ponto de atenção" on an NC row of a ficha or a local, "Criar ponto" on the Não ensaiado band, "Criar" on the Points screen. The layout-lock goldens v5, v11 and v12 were regenerated (their section 8 now prints its empty note); the Porto Seguro skeleton golden was not, pending Matheus (spec e24h D3). Contract 41 was reserved for e24a; the coordinator renumbers the unlaunched batches.)*

*(2026-11-10, fix batch e24h, H9 (Bruno through Matheus): an observation never forces a restriction, on every seed. `relatorio/conclusion.ts` `computeFloor` lost the `observation` reason ("observação registrada"): the sheet's OBSERVAÇÕES or a C/NA row's observation is a comment, the floor stays Aprovado · Sem restrições and Sem restrições stays choosable; Com restrições, when the technician picks it, still requires OBSERVAÇÕES. Every other floor source is unchanged; no stored pair is rewritten. This is the "an observation floors nothing" half Story 24.3 had planned for v14 only.)*

*(2026-11-10, fix batch e24f (contract 42, minimum 42; the Epic 23 retrospective's R-1, R-3 and R-5). R-1, the paragraph rule: `composeConclusion` says "valores medidos com leitura a conferir", never "dentro dos critérios de aceitação", while a measured reading was judged by no criterion: a live measurement test the deviation between phases left `unjudged` (each such cell carries `UNJUDGED_DEVIATION_HELPER`, verdict null, not `toCheck`), or, beside a judged capture, a seeded test with no criterion holding a measured capture (the floor's Story 14.9 case); with nothing judged the "sem comparação" text stays; an out clause still comes first; the basis is unchanged, so a text confirmed before keeps printing. R-3: `setDraftTestRecommendation` also deletes the test's `index_recommendation`. R-5: `applyOp` takes a clear (a put of null, a remove) on a TTR row off the plate whatever the row holds, and on a row with no filled cell it changes nothing; a value there stays refused. The web client ends a JSON request whose answer's headers never come as a network failure after `requestHeadersTimeoutMs` (60 s plus the body at 8 KB/s; the download is not cut), so a lost answer no longer holds the single-flight sync cycle and the Export dialog's revision poll.)*
