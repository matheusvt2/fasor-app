# Seed v11 and v12

Earlier versions: [seed-versions](/architecture/seed-versions.md). Next: [criteria-and-judgments](/architecture/criteria-and-judgments.md) (v13). The conclusion, points and parecer rules of the same epic (batch e22c) are in [conclusion-points-parecer](/architecture/conclusion-points-parecer.md); the plate reading itself is in [suggestions-and-reading](/architecture/suggestions-and-reading.md). The dated notes below were moved unchanged from `kernel-and-seed.md` on 2026-11-28; a note marked "planning, not built" is kept as history beside the "as built" note that followed it.

*(2026-11-05, Stories 21.1 and 21.2 (contract 30, minimum 30): `SEED_VERSION` is `v11` (`seed/v11.ts`, derived from v10, pinned by `V10_HASH`): the transformador de força's LIGAÇÃO SECUNDÁRIA is a select of the 47 vector groups of `relatorio/vector-group.ts` (`VECTOR_GROUPS`, `parseVectorGroup`, the factor k, the terminal pairs and shorts per phase, from the research note `research/ttr-vector-groups-2026-11-05/research.md`) and its ratio table's `ratio_factor` is `vector_group`. `ratioGroupOf` (`relatorio/reading-cells.ts`) resolves the group: on v11 the parsed one or none (no VAL CALCULADO, `groupMissing`, one Ensaios missing per row); on v9/v10 (`sqrt3`) the parsed one, else Dyn1 with a screen helper. The capture labels (`VisibleColumn.label`, `EvaluatedCell.column`) and section 9's capture headers read the group's pairs; storage stays by column index. `selectOptionFor` reads a value onto the vector-group select through the parser. A plate reading of the group cited by no printed run is a diagram read: `verify`, `ocr_token_ids: []`, the whole image, and `plateSuggestionNoteText` gives the device's note. 2026-11-05, fix batch e21f: the model no longer composes a drawn group; it describes the drawing (`vector_diagram` facts in the structuring tool, prompt `bedrock-structuring-4`) and `composeVectorGroup` (`relatorio/vector-group.ts`) composes the code, folded into the group's value by the api provider before the contract parse.)*

*(2026-11-06, fix batch e21g (`spec-e21g-review-notes.md`): while a v11 table waits for its group its captures read "Fase 1" to "Fase 3" (`vectorGroupNeutralCaptureLabel`, printed "FASE 1" to "FASE 3"), a v9/v10 table with an empty LIGAÇÃO SECUNDÁRIA says `VECTOR_GROUP_EMPTY_TEXT` (`RatioGroupResolution.blank`), no Sem restrições is suggested over a missing group, and `sheetJumpTarget` (`relatorio/sheet-progress.ts`, with the `steps.ensaios.group` share) sends "Concluir ficha" to the LIGAÇÃO SECUNDÁRIA field when the group is all Ensaios misses.)*

*(2026-11-06, design rule from the Epic 21 retrospective, P-2 (`epic-batch-orchestrator.md` § 10): a reading prompt that needs a derived value (a group code, a class, a verdict) asks the model for the perceptual facts, and a kernel function composes the value, as `composeVectorGroup` does for the drawn vector diagram; asked for the code the model answered Dy1 3 of 3, asked for the facts it was right 5 of 5. Fix batch e21a (`spec-e21a-reading-fixes.md`): `vectorGroupReadOf` (`reading/build.ts`) is the one classification of a structured vector-group value (printed, other group, diagram, unparsed, dropped) that the job, the plate fold and the eval read; `parseVectorGroup` reads a single trailing I or l as clock 1 ("Dyn I", as a real plate prints it).)*

*(2026-11-06, Stories 22.7 to 22.9 and 22.11 as built, batch e22s (contract 32, minimum 32): `SEED_VERSION` is `v12` (`seed/v12.ts`, derived from v11, pinned by `V11_HASH`). TIPO DE ISOLAÇÃO offers ÓLEO MINERAL on the transformador de força, TP and TC (`option_aliases` reads an earlier ÓLEO as it, the oil rule's `oil_values`); the transformador de força gains TIPO DO ÓLEO (A, B, an oil field) and labels its vector-group select DESLOCAMENTO ANGULAR; the TP takes the TC's v10 list (VASAMENTO its one oil item); the Não ensaiado reason `defeito_no_equipamento` is the first flagged `defect`. The seed schema gains optional keys only: a column role `voltage` with `options`, a table's `voltage_rows` (`default_kv`, `winding`) and a test's `criterion_class_field`. Every v12 insulation table appends a TENSÃO (kV) column (stored indices as v11's); `relatorio/row-voltage.ts` `rowTestVoltage` gives each row's effective voltage (typed cell, else the row's default, else the instrument's TENSÃO ENSAIO when it is an option) and its screen-only notice (a winding rated at most 1000 V above 1 kV, OQ-1), exposed as `EvaluatedRow.voltage` and `EvaluatedTable.voltageColumn`, never a value column; section 9 prints it right after the connection columns. `relatorio/accuracy-class.ts` `parseAccuracyClass` and `accuracyClassDeviation` make the TC and TP ratio criterion ± the class % (5P, 10P ± 3 %), the seeded ± 0,5 % with `ACCURACY_CLASS_HELPER` while EXATIDÃO is empty, `criterion_override` still winning; the plate reading passes an EXATIDÃO select value through the parser and composes TIPO DE ISOLAÇÃO from a TIPO DO ÓLEO read alone (`verify`, prompt `bedrock-structuring-5`). One library type, `inspecao_qgbt` (TAG `QGBT`, four fields, the Quadro elétrico's ten visual items, no tests), prints in "Outros equipamentos" after its run's last Quadro elétrico (`group-for-print.ts` `afterFullQuadros`).)* *(2026-11-07, fix batch e22g (`spec-e22g-wave-b-fixes.md`), seed v12 changed in place before its first deploy (`V12_HASH` re-pinned): from Bruno's test base rev03 the protection classes are TC 5P ± 1 % and 10P ± 3 %, TP 3P ± 3 % and 6P ± 6 % (the TP's EXATIDÃO gains 3P and 6P); `parseAccuracyClass` reads the old NBR ("0,6C25", "10B100", "0,3P75") and the IEC ("25VA 5P20", "12,5VA 0,3") notations, keyed on the field (`ACCURACY_CLASS_FIELD`) in `selectOptionFor`; `rowTestVoltage` takes `ratingDefaultKv` (a winding above 600 V: 2,5 kV to 5000 V, 5 kV above) before the instrument's TENSÃO ENSAIO; the Inspeção de QGBT prints as INSPEÇÃO DE QGBT. Over-range: `normalizeOhmUnit` reads an instrument limit's typed unit as an Ω-table key, `overRangeMissingLimitText` says when its unit is unreadable, a unit carry-over skips an `over_range` row, and `ops/value-limits.ts` `plain_cell` refuses `over_range` on a nameplate field and a measurement test value. Section 9: `fittedColumns` steps a reading table wider than `READING_FIT_WIDTH_EM` down to the largest of 8,5 to 7 pt at which every word is whole (`PrintTable.textHalfPoints`, applied by the DOCX renderer), and MANUT. ANTERIOR is decided per table.)*
