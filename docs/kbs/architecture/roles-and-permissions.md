# Roles and permissions

- **Kernel (one verdict)**: `packages/domain/src/permissions/`. `keys.ts` the twelve keys (`PERMISSION_KEYS`) and their modules, with the pt-BR module names and key labels. `roles.ts` the `role` row (`roleRowSchema`: `id`, `name`, `permissions[]`, `system?`, `removed_at?`), the five seeded roles (`SYSTEM_ROLE_IDS`) under constant row ids (`SYSTEM_ROLE_ROW_IDS`, the same in every company), their sets (`SYSTEM_ROLE_PERMISSIONS`, the epic's table) and `systemRoleRows()`. `can.ts` holds `can(role, key)`, `keysOf` and `isAdminRole`. `isAdminRole` is the one "Admin" condition: the seeded Admin row holds every key whatever is stored. No role means no key, and a removed role grants nothing. `map.ts` holds `permissionForPath(path, op)` (typed over every path family; `relatorio/status` depends on its value; `SETUP_CREATE_FIELDS` and `SETUP_FILL_FIELDS` split `relatorio/setup`; `FICHA_REGISTRY_KINDS`), `permissionForRoute(name)` and `PERMISSION_ROUTES`. `user.ts` holds the statuses (`active`, `invited`, `paused`, `removed`), `activeUser` (an absent status counts as active) and their words. `last-admin.ts` holds `lastAdminVerdict(users, change)`.
- **Data**: the `role` entity is company scope. The user row gains the optional `role_id` and `status`. The families `role`, `role/field`, `user/role_id` and `user/status` are all server-only (actor `system:admin`), excluded from `USER_FIELDS`, and reach devices by the company pull. Contract 55.
- **Server**: `apps/api/src/db/roles.ts` holds `ensureSystemRoles` (run by every `seedUser`) and `upgradeRolesAndUsers` (the boot and `migrate-cli` data step: the five roles per company, then every user without a role becomes an active Admin; idempotent). The push's `checkClientOp` (`sync/rules.ts`) refuses `op_forbidden` when the actor's role lacks the op's key; the role is read once per push. `http/session.ts` puts `role` and `status` on `SessionContext` on every request. `requireSession` answers `401 user_inactive` for a user who is not active, and `requirePermission(c, key)` answers `403 forbidden`. The routes use `permissionForRoute`: generate, preview, preview PDF, audit and reread need `relatorio.issue`; a revision's DOCX or PDF needs `export.download`; an upload needs `relatorio.fill`; a file read needs `relatorio.view`. The better-auth session hook (`auth/auth.ts`) refuses the sign-in of a paused or removed user with `user_inactive`. *(2026-11-22, Story 26.11: a pause or a remove keeps the user's sessions and ends only his pending password links; every route, and better-auth's get-session through a guard in `http/app.ts`, answers `401 user_inactive` for a user who is not active, so a kept session is inert and the device learns the cause; a reactivation makes the kept sessions work again; a password reset still ends sessions. Test: 26.11-API-001.)*
- **The Admin's routes (2026-11-21, batch e26c, Story 26.8)**: `/api/admin/users` (`contract/admin-users.ts`, `ADMIN_USER_ROUTES`): `GET` the list; `POST` create `{name, email, role_id}` (born `invited`, the invitation sent); `PATCH /:id` `{name?, role_id?}`; `POST /:id/{pause|reactivate|remove|resend-invitation|password-reset}` with `{}`. All need `users.manage` (`permissionForRoute('admin.users')`). The service `apps/api/src/admin/users.ts` runs each change as one `applyServerBatch` whose `before` decides again under the company lock (tenant `404 user_not_found`, `user_state_invalid`, `role_not_found`, `lastAdminVerdict` `409 last_admin`) and makes the table writes; a duplicate e-mail, a removed user's included, is `409 user_email_taken` (`details` `{user_id, status}` only for the caller's company). Pause only from active; reactivate from paused or removed; remove from active, invited or paused; resend only for invited; reset for active or invited. Create, resend and reset share one limit per Admin (30 per 10 minutes). Routes `http/admin-users.ts`.
- **Device**: `rejectionCause(row, role)` (`sync/counts.ts`) names an `op_forbidden` the viewer's role explains as `OP_FORBIDDEN_BY_ROLE`, and Sync status shows its own sentence (`opRejectReasonText`). The viewer's role is `localRoleOf` in `apps/web/src/db/sync-store.ts`. Hiding by role is Story 26.5.
- **Tests and seeds**: every existing test user is Admin. The e2e role users (one per seeded role but Admin) are `workerRoleUser(index, role)` in each worker's Empresa B. They are seeded on demand by `seedRoleUser`, never at the global setup (see [api-app](/code/api-app.md)). `scripts/seed-users.ts --role <system id>`.
- **Open readings (Matheus)**: `suggestion/status` needs `relatorio.fill`, and no op needs `suggestion.review` yet. The ficha-created `manufacturer` and `voltage_class` need `relatorio.fill` on create only. The `relatorio/setup` creation-form fields need `relatorio.create` and the field ones `relatorio.fill`. `relatorio/export/scheme` needs `relatorio.issue`. A role change takes effect on the next request.
