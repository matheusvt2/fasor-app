# Seed v15 to v19

Earlier: [seed-v14](/architecture/seed-v14.md). A version opens equal to the one before, takes its changes in fenced sections that a `without…` helper in `seed/seed.test.ts` can strip, and freezes at its deploy. The dated notes below were moved unchanged from `kernel-and-seed.md` on 2026-11-28; a note marked "planning, not built" is kept as history beside the "as built" note that followed it.

*(2026-11-11, planning, not built: Epic 25 (`epic-25-context.md`).*

- *Seed v15 derives from v14 (`V14_HASH` pinned as shipped) and opens only after the Epic 24 deploy has frozen v14. Story 25.5 creates it equal to v14 and adds v14 goldens to the layout lock; later batches add to it in place until its first deploy.* *(2026-11-11: the v14 goldens exist since batch e25b, PR #325: `golden/layout-lock-v14/`, generated from the code deployed as `2009fa30`. Story 25.5 keeps them byte-equal and adds a second v14 fixture with its own golden.)*
- *On v15, each through a definition key or a flag of the seed-level `rules`: a sheet with no measured reading and at least one "Não medido" does not accept Aprovado (25.6); the conclusion paragraph names a Reprovado by hand and the tests left unmeasured (25.7); a contact rise equal to the limit is restricted (25.8); a reading cell takes N/A (25.9); a point started from a test names every reading out of its criterion (25.10); a measurement test may take rows the technician adds, the one fold change of the epic (25.14); the thermography asks "Realizada" or "Não realizada" with points by type (25.15); the No-break de comando gains tests (25.17); measurement tables print a criterion and a result per row and the corrected headers say "(corrigido)" (25.20); the fixed texts, the cover's place and the validity line change (25.22).*
- *On every seed: a field that unmounts commits what it holds (25.2); a commit asks for a sync cycle (25.3); the revision row keeps the verdicts it printed, and the stored pair and parecer stay the technician's choice (25.4); `conclusion_stale` becomes a blocking pre-issue row (25.7); three more pending rows before generating (25.12).*
- *Contract numbers are taken at each merge. Main is at 47.)* *(2026-11-11: 48 after batch e25b, 49 after fix batch e25r.)*
