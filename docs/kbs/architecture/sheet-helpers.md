# Sheet helpers on every seed

These batches changed no seed data, with the exception of three v14 `rules` flags of batch e24y. The v14 seed itself: [seed-v14](/architecture/seed-v14.md). The dated notes below were moved unchanged from `kernel-and-seed.md` on 2026-11-28; a note marked "planning, not built" is kept as history beside the "as built" note that followed it.

*(2026-11-11, Stories 24.5 to 24.9 and 24.21 as built, batch e24b, contract 43 with minimum 43, `spec-e24b-screen-lane.md`; every seed, no seed change, no golden moved.)*

- *The TAG (24.8): `relatorio/tag.ts` `suggestTag(type, equipment, definition)` gives PREFIX-NN, the smallest number free among the obra's live TAGs read as `PREFIX-<digits>` ("TR-1" holds 1), two digits up to 99; the seccionadora's prefix is SC. The rule before is frozen as `legacySuggestTag(type, location, equipment, definition)` with `LEGACY_TAG_PREFIX` (SEC); nothing on screen shows it. `moveTagSuggestion()` is always null: a move offers no rename.*
- *The reuse key (24.21): the equipment row gains the optional, write-once `reuse_key` (never blank, no put path). `instantiateTemplate` gives each placement the key `legacySuggestTag` gives it over the keys this run produced, binds the live row of its type whose stored key equals it, else a row with no key whose TAG equals it (the smallest id wins a tie), else creates the row with the key and the PREFIX-NN TAG. `newEquipmentBlock` takes an optional `placement` and writes `reuseKeyFor(type, location, equipment)`, free among the obra's match keys (`equipmentMatchKey`: the key, else the TAG). `InstantiateInputs.tagRule: 'legacy'` builds the TAGs of the rule before with no key: the layout-lock, Porto Seguro and replay fixtures and `e2e/support/relatorio-seed.ts` pass it.*
- *The conclusion preview (24.5): `conclusionPreview(block, definition, visit)` says which half shows the floor as a preview (tested sheet, a floor, the half's cell unfilled); nothing prints from it.*
- *The NC phrase (24.6): `ncObservationOnLeave(from, to, observation, chips)` gives `clear` (only offered phrases, a final period aside, `typedPieces`), `keep` (a typed piece: the row's warning) or null; it runs on NC to C and NC to N.A. A phrase the server learned from the row's own observation after its push is not offered for that judgement (`learnedBefore`). `ncChipPhrases` compares without the final period.*
- *The point question (24.7): `pointsAskedOnLeave(points, owner, itemKey, from, to)` lists the live points of origin `nc` with the row's `item_key` and owner; `pointQuestionText` words the ConfirmDialog. The row writes its result, the observation clear and the removals in one batch.*
- *Small fixes (24.9): `tapsHelperText` names the separator; `thermalPhotoText` counts the sheet's shots by the thermography's context caption; `evaluation.fallbackLines` (the fallback sentences outside "Fonte do critério"); `screenCriterionText` ("Óleo: … · Seco: …" while the medium is unknown, the printed `criterionText` unchanged); "Critérios usados" names a contact rise; `unreadableTemplatesText` (`templates/list.ts`).*

*(2026-11-11, fix batch e24x, the integrated review's fixes part 1 and Matheus's decision 17, `spec-e24x-review-fixes.md`; no contract, no golden moved.)*

- *The NC phrase, amended: `ncObservationOnLeave(from, to, stored, seedPhrases, typed = stored)` clears only an observation made of the item's seed phrases alone (a learned or a recent phrase counts as typed); a text still being saved (`typed` ahead of the store) is `keep` when non-blank, null when blank. `learnedBefore` is gone; the row target carries `seedPhrases`. The kept warning outlives leaving the ficha and a reload (`sessionStorage`, `ficha:kept:` keys, cleared at sign-out).*
- *Over the instrument's limit on a range: `overRangeVerdict` reads `value_max`; a `between` limit at or above the upper end is `out` (its excess measured from there), else null; such a null reading withholds the floor and reads "a conferir" (a reading table's capture through `conclusion.ts`, a measurement test's through its `unjudged`). The group's "a conferir" clause needs a measured capture in the waiting table. A v14 NC line ending in ":" joins the next with a space. `worstReadings` ranks a range criterion's readings by their distance outside the nearer end.*
- *`ops/value-limits.ts`: the equipment create's `reuse_key` is medium (1 000). `types/palette-meta.ts`: `paletteMetaText` composes both palettes' meta line ("Tipo da empresa" alone with no part); `composerHiddenTypes` gives the composer the hidden originals a template places (right after their copies, "Editar padrões", a disabled stepper). The web client's uplink floor is 1 KB/s (`requestHeadersTimeoutMs`), the body's share capped at 300 s (`SYNC_REQUEST_BODY_SHARE_MAX_MS`, the server's request limit), so one attempt waits at most 360 s.*
- *Additions D1 to D5 (same batch): the point editor opened from a test's place or the Não ensaiado band stays open when its autosave stores the point (`CreatePointAction` `hideButton`, the action kept mounted). On v14 a joined action over the medium cap starts empty, the recommendations then starting the text (Story 23.11's form) or, past the long cap, the finding alone. `previousResultsFor(…, visitDate)` never reads a visit later than the relatório's own (`relatorioVisitDate`: `service_end`, else `service_start`; the top's prior when not later, else nothing; no date, as before), passed by `visitContextOf` (`source.relatorio`), section 9 and `previousHelpers`. A `reading` and a `restricao` point of one test count as that test's point (`testSource`).*
- *E1 (after merging e24s): a type with no seeded test whose every measurement test applies only on its own answer and is not live on the sheet has nothing to measure (`nothingToMeasure`): the v14 Inspeção de QGBT with its thermography off, fora de carga or unanswered reads as v13, without ", sem valores medidos registrados"; v1 to v13 unchanged.*
